## Conformal geodesics and the hyperboloid at timelike infinity

Achilleas V. Koukas、Mariem Magdy、Juan A. Valiente Kroon

以共形测地线和共形 Gaussian 规范，将 Minkowski 时空的类时无穷远点扩展为与类光无穷远相交的双曲面边界。

A description of scattering in asymptotically flat spacetimes that includes both radiation and massive asymptotic states requires control of the relation between null and timelike infinity. Motivated by this problem, we use the properties of conformal geodesics to construct an extended representation of timelike infinity in Minkowski spacetime. The construction exploits the fact that every non-null metric geodesic in an Einstein spacetime is, up to an explicit local reparametrisation, a conformal geodesic. It replaces the point $i^+$ by a hyperboloidal boundary $\mathcal{H}^+$ meeting future null infinity $\mathscr{I}^+$ at a corner $\mathcal{C}^{+}$, while fixing the location of $\mathscr{I}^+$ in the associated conformal Gaussian gauge. In this gauge, we derive the equations governing massless scalar and spin-$2$ fields. In particular, we obtain a transport equation for the scalar field along future null infinity and analyse the asymptotic behaviour of its spherical-harmonic modes near timelike infinity. We also derive the explicit massless spin-$2$ equations in this gauge. Finally, we examine the relation to projective extensions and to the hyperbolic base of the extended Carrollian space $\mathrm{Ti}$. Our results provide a geometric framework for studying the connection between late-time and null asymptotics which is a prerequisite for scattering constructions relevant to flat space holography.

## Sphere decomposition for finite subgroups of 3-manifold mapping class groups

Corey Bregman、Bena Tshishiku

对闭定向三维流形，研究映射类群有限子群与填充球面系统的相容性。

For a closed, oriented 3-manifold $M$, we show that any finite subgroup of its mapping class group preserves an isotopy class of filling sphere system on $M$. This is an isotopy version of the "equivariant sphere theorem" of Meeks-Yau. As part of our analysis, we establish a bijection between simplices of the Guirardel--Levitt outer space for $\pi_1(M)$ and filling sphere systems in $M$, generalizing an argument of Hatcher. We also compute the image of $\mathrm{Homeo}(M)\to\mathrm{Out}(\pi_1M)$ for any such $M$.

## The Calabi-Yau and Sullivan conjectures for minimal surfaces with symmetries

Antonio Alarcon、Franc Forstneric

在有限群等变条件下，构造带边 Riemann 曲面内部到欧氏空间的 proper 共形极小浸入，以及到单位球的完备 proper 浸入。

Let \(G\) be a finite group acting on a connected open Riemann surface \(X\) by holomorphic automorphisms and acting on a Euclidean space \(\mathbb R^n\), \(n\ge 3\), by orthogonal transformations. In a previous paper, the second author gave a necessary and sufficient condition for the existence of a \(G\)-equivariant conformal minimal immersion \(F:X\to\mathbb R^n\), and he asked whether such \(F\) can be chosen to be a proper map. Assuming that this condition holds, we give an affirmative answer when \(X\) is a bordered Riemann surface and \(G\) acts freely on the boundary of \(X\). This contributes to the Sullivan conjecture concerning the existence of proper hyperbolic minimal surfaces in \(\mathbb R^3\) of finite topology. Under the same conditions, we also construct a \(G\)-equivariant proper conformal minimal immersion of \(X\) to the unit ball of \(\mathbb R^n\) which is complete in the induced metric, thereby opening a new direction in the Calabi--Yau theory regarding the study of complete bounded minimal surfaces. Analogous results hold for null curves in \(\mathbb C^n\).

## Geometric Quantities in Spray Geometry and Their Applications to Finsler Geometry

综述 spray 几何中的 S-曲率、χ-曲率和若干 Ricci 曲率概念，重新解释其恒等式及 Finsler 应用。

In this paper we give an overview of several developments in spray geometry and provide some further interpretations of the geometric quantities that arise naturally in this subject. ## The Pu inequality and the Viterbo conjecture with finite symmetry

Julian Chaidez、Shira Tanny

通过四元数辛覆盖与可逆 Finsler RP² 的 Pu 不等式，证明 R⁴ 中 Q 对称凸体满足尖锐 Viterbo 不等式；光滑等号体辛同胚于球。

We study a construction for producing Finsler metrics on the sphere from convex bodies in $\mathbb{R}^4$ using the quaternions. Among several applications, we prove the weak Viterbo conjecture for convex bodies in $\mathbb{R}^4$ that are invariant under the natural action of the quaternion group $Q$. This is the first instance where Viterbo's systolic inequality is established for convex bodies with a finite group symmetry, addressing a question of Haim-Kislev-Ostrover. Finally, we show that any link of minimal Reeb orbits on the boundary of a uniformly convex $Q$-symmetric convex body is transversely isotopic to a union of Hopf fibers, strengthening a result of Abbondandolo-Edtmair-Kang in the $Q$-symmetric setting.

## Singular Loci of SL2(C)-Representation Varieties of Torus Knot Groups

S. Liriano

计算互素参数环面结群的 SL₂(C) 表示簇之奇异集的整系数同调。

Let $T(q,p)=\langle x,y\mid x^p=y^q\rangle$, where $p,q>1$ are relatively prime, and let \[ R=\Hom(T(q,p),\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb C)). \] We compute the integral homology of the singular locus of $R$. We show that this locus has complex dimension $2$ and is a disjoint union of $\mu=(p-1)(q-1)$ smooth affine surfaces, each homotopy equivalent to the two-dimensional sphere. Consequently, \[ H_0(\Sing R;\Z)\cong H_2(\Sing R;\Z)\cong\Z^\mu, \] and all remaining homology groups vanish. Thus both nonzero Betti numbers equal the first Betti number of the Milnor fiber of the plane curve singularity $z^p+w^q=0$, namely its Milnor number $\mu$.

## Gagliardo--Nirenberg extremals on manifolds with small holes

Mayukh Mukherjee、Utsab Sarkar

研究闭流形挖去小容量洞后，齐次 Dirichlet Gagliardo–Nirenberg 商的极值、尖锐常数和稳定性。

We study the sharp homogeneous Dirichlet Gagliardo--Nirenberg quotient on a closed Riemannian manifold with holes of small capacity and connected complements. For each fixed exponent $2<q<2^*$, the nonnegative $L^2$-normalized extremal exists and is unique when the first Dirichlet eigenvalue $\lambda_D$ is sufficiently small. If the ambient volume is $V$ and $\beta=n(q-2)/2$, the sharp constant satisfies $C_D=V^{1-q/2}\lambda_D^{-\beta/2}(1+O(\lambda_D))$. The constants depend only on the ambient manifold and the exponent. On general compact manifolds with Dirichlet boundary conditions, we prove compactness above the Euclidean sharp constant and describe concentration at equality. Positive scalar curvature at one interior point implies attainment. For the $L^2$-critical nonlinear Schrödinger energy, the mass threshold for negative energy tends to zero as the capacity of the holes tends to zero, whereas the threshold for unboundedness below is unchanged.

## Sobolev inequalities for sections and projections of log-concave functions

Luis J. Alías、Bernardo González Merino、Beatriz Marín Gimeno

为对数凹函数的截面和投影建立高阶尖锐 Sobolev 与局部 Sobolev 型不等式。

Higher order sharp Sobolev and local Sobolev type inequalities for log-concave functions are established. The new inequalities directly imply the isoperimetric inequalities by Bollobás and Thomason in 1995, by Liakopoulos in 2019, and the recently discovered local isoperimetric inequalities from [1]. In order to do so, we make use of three ingredients. First, sets of functional inequalities proven by Alonso-Gutiérrez, Bernués, Brazitikos and Carbery in 2021. Second, a multidimensional polynomial optimization result that originated in [1] and was initiated by Bollobás and Thomason. Third, a new integral formula for the projection of a log-concave function that links the geometric part with the more functional versions via some dependence on the Hessian matrix restricted to subspaces.
