## Hodge--Riemann relations for nef bundles

Enhan Li、Weizhe Zheng

在Hodge–Lefschetz模中引入(m,p,q)-Lefschetz性质，研究紧Kähler流形上nef类及nef向量丛特征类。Hall–Rado型条件下，作者证明顶Chern类乘积及适当双次数中导出Schur类乘积的Hodge–Riemann关系。

We introduce the $(m,p,q)$-Lefschetz property for $(1,1)$-type endomorphisms of Hodge--Lefschetz modules, providing a framework for studying nef $(1,1)$-classes and characteristic classes of nef vector bundles on compact Kähler manifolds. Under a Hall--Rado type condition on nef twisted vector bundles, we prove Hodge--Riemann relations for products of top Chern classes and, in suitable bidegrees, for products of derived Schur classes. For products of nef $(1,1)$-classes, we show furthermore that the Hall--Rado type condition is equivalent to simultaneous Hodge--Riemann relations. Our proofs build on the purity theorem and descent lemma of Cattani, Kaplan, and Schmid. We extend the descent lemma to Schur classes of nef vector bundles. We also establish formal analogs for projective bundle modules over Hodge--Lefschetz modules, with applications to tautological classes and descent on matroid Chow rings.

## Braided Multisections and Akhmedov's Fake Quadrics

Anar Akhmedov

为Σ_g×S²中既有循环多截面的补空间给把柄分解，按乘积坐标确定2-把柄标架，并从链复形计算整同调，包括H₂扭转。

We give handlebody decompositions of the complements of the cyclic multisections in $\Sigma_g\times S^2$ constructed in~\cite{AB}. The 2-handle framings are induced by the product coordinates. We compute the integral homology of the complements from their handle chain complexes, including the torsion subgroup of $H_2$. For the degree pairs $(3,3)$ and $(2,4)$, we specify surface diffeomorphisms inducing the maps on first homology prescribed in~\cite{AY} and recover the integral first homology groups $(\Z/3)^2\oplus\Z/(3m)$ and $(\Z/2)^2\oplus\Z/(4m)$, respectively, for $m\geq1$. For the symmetric $m=1$ sum, we also give a simultaneous surface diagram of the relative product attachments and identify their product framings with the surface framings. The resulting symplectic sums are aspherical and symplectically minimal, and have the rational cohomology ring of $S^2\times S^2$.

## Coupled free energy and a diagonal approach to coupled Kähler-Einstein metrics

Takahiro Aoi、Satoshi Nakamura

As a consequence of the regularization process of the non-Archimedean entropy established by Boucksom-Jonsson, Chi Li and Trusiani, we prove that the existence of a unique coupled Kähler-Einstein metric is equivalent to the stability condition that there exists $\epsilon >0$ such that $F_{-1-\epsilon}^{\rm cp,NA} \geq 0$ on the space of measures associated with a test configuration for the anticanonical line bundle. As a coupled version of the work by Berman, we show that the law of the empirical measure satisfies a large deviation principle whose rate function is the coupled free energy under the assumption that a conjecture by Hultgren is true. We also introduce a coupled version of the microscopic stability thresholds and prove that a uniformly coupled Gibbs stable Fano manifold has a unique coupled Kähler-Einstein metric. This is a generalization of the results by Fujita-Odaka and Berman.

## Classification of real Lie algebras of dimension five

Mehdi Nadjafikhah

用Levi–Malcev分解及幂零根基分层总结五维实Lie代数的同构分类，避免直接求解模GL(5,R)的Jacobi方程组。

We summarize a coordinate-free classification of five-dimensional real Lie algebras up to isomorphism. Rather than solving the overdetermined quadratic Jacobi system modulo $\mathrm{GL}(5,\mathbb{R})$, the method uses Levi--Malcev theory and nilradical stratification. In the non-solvable case, the Levi factor is three-dimensional, $\mathfrak{so}(3)$ or $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R})$, acting on a two-dimensional radical; this yields exactly five classes, including the indecomposable $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R})\ltimes\mathbb{R}^2$. In the solvable case, Schur's bound on commutative subalgebras of $\mathfrak{gl}(2,\mathbb{R})$ implies $\dim\mathfrak{n}\in\{3,4,5\}$, eliminating one- and two-dimensional nilradicals. The nilpotent case $\dim\mathfrak{n}=5$ has nine classes: three decomposable and six indecomposable, including $\mathfrak{h}_5$ and two filiform algebras. For $\dim\mathfrak{n}=4$, classification reduces to normal forms of outer derivations for $\mathbb{R}^4$, $\mathfrak{h}_3\oplus\mathbb{R}$, and $\mathfrak{n}_4$ under automorphism and scaling. For $\dim\mathfrak{n}=3$, it uses two-dimensional abelian subalgebras of $\mathrm{OutDer}(\mathfrak{n})$ containing no nonzero nilpotent operators. The work also computes minimal faithful representation dimensions $\mu(\mathfrak{g})$, gives a dictionary with classical tables, and designs a deterministic identification algorithm.

## On a Dual Heterotic Kodaira-Spencer Theory

Siddhartha Mangamuri、Eirik Eik Svanes、Anuthi Tiwary

在紧Calabi–Yau三维空间、tr(F²)上同调平凡且仅保留规范反常的情形，把Green–Schwarz机制放进经典变形复形，构造作用于T¹⁰X⊕End(V)⊕T*¹⁰X的幂零算子D̄。

We consider a dual version of heterotic Kodaira-Spencer theory on a Calabi-Yau three-fold $X$ with background fluxes, related to the type I BCOV theory of Costello-Li. We implement the Green-Schwarz mechanism of Costello-Li, where the Beltrami differential plays the role of the Green-Schwarz field. On a compact Calabi-Yau three-fold with background gauge fluxes $F$, where $\mathrm{tr}(F^2)$ is trivial in cohomology, retaining gauge anomalies only and leaving gravitational anomalies to future work, we incorporate the Costello-Li Green-Schwarz mechanism into the classical deformation complex. This gives a theory governed by a nilpotent operator $\bar D$ on $Q=T^{1,0}X\oplus\operatorname{End}(V)\oplus T^{*1,0}X$, whose extension maps carry holomorphic derivatives, so that $(Q,\bar D)$ is not a holomorphic bundle. Using diagonal $\bar\partial^\dagger$-gauge, the one-loop gauge anomaly cancels, assuming that the extension maps of $\bar D$ do not modify it, which we argue is plausible. Introducing an auxiliary $(0,2)$-form, we construct a cubic interacting theory whose gauge symmetries are governed by an $L_3$ algebra. On compact Calabi-Yau three-folds, and for sufficiently small fields, we show that the equation of motion is equivalent to the Maurer-Cartan equation, together with a divergence-free constraint on the Beltrami differential. Finally we trivialise $\bar D$ locally to the Dolbeault operator. The (non-tensorial) transition maps glue into a quasi-holomorphic extension sheaf $\widetilde{\mathscr{Q}}$, for which we prove a Dolbeault theorem, $H^{0,q}_{\bar D}(Q)\cong\check H^q(\widetilde{\mathscr{Q}})$.

## Measures of maximal entropy for monotone factors of smooth surface diffeomorphisms

Sylvain Crovisier、Alejo García-Sassi、Yuri Lima、Fábio Tal

对光滑曲面微分同胚的拓扑传递单调因子，若其拓扑熵为正，作者证明存在唯一极大熵测度。因子熵来自单个同宿类，该类每个正熵遍历测度上因子映射为测度同构。

We prove that a transitive monotone factor of a smooth surface diffeomorphism has a unique measure of maximal entropy whenever its topological entropy is positive. Its entropy is the entropy of a single homoclinic class of the diffeomorphism, and the factor map is a measure-theoretic isomorphism on every ergodic measure with positive entropy of that class. ## Holomorphic sectional curvature and the Calabi operator on Kähler C-spaces

Xiaolong Li、Peter Petersen

The proofs involve an integral identity for holomorphic sectional curvature, projective embeddings, root-space estimates, and explicit metrics on projective bundles.

## On do Carmo's Question: The Properly Embedded Case

Gongping Niu

In this paper, we prove that every complete noncompact two-sided properly embedded constant mean curvature hypersurface of finite Morse index in Euclidean space is minimal. The result holds in every dimension. We assume no bounds on curvature or volume growth. This establishes the properly embedded case of the finite-index version of do Carmo's question.
