## Gromov hyperbolicity and uniformity

Qingshan Zhou

作者构造适当测地度量空间中的可求长连通开集，使其拟双曲度量为Gromov双曲，视觉边界与原度量边界自然双Lipschitz对应，但开集仍非一致域。随后在附加假设下给一致性同Gromov双曲及自然闭包映射相对拟对称性的等价刻画。

In this paper, we first exhibit an example which answers negatively to a question posed by Herron et al. in 2008. That is, there is a rectifiably connected open set in a proper geodesic metric space so that it is Gromov hyperbolic with respect to the quasihyperbolic metric, and its Gromov boundary endowed with a visual metric is naturally bilipschitz equivalent to its metric boundary, but it is not uniform. Moreover, two positive answers are given under additional hypotheses. It is shown that a metric space is uniform if and only if it is Gromov hyperbolic and the natural map between its Gromov closure and its metric completion exists and satisfies a relatively quasisymmetric condition. Both bounded and unbounded cases are in our considerations via two different uniformization theorems developed recently by Bonk et al. and the first author, respectively. Finally, we characterize uniform metric spaces with uniformly perfect boundaries in terms of Gromov hyperbolicity and Teichmüller's displacement property.

## Isosceles sets in Euclidean spaces of dimension nine to twenty-two

Hyunjun Yi

研究任意三点组成等腰三角形的有限欧氏点集。作者给9≤d≤21时最大点数为C(d+1,2)+1，22维为276，并分类相应极值构型。

A finite set of points in $\mathbb{R}^d$ is isosceles if every three of its points form an isosceles triangle. Erdős asked in 1946 for the largest size $\mathrm{iso}(d)$ of an isosceles set in $\mathbb{R}^d$. The answer was known for $d\le 8$, the last cases being due to Ionin. We prove that $\mathrm{iso}(d)=\binom{d+1}{2}+1$ for $9\le d\le 21$ and $\mathrm{iso}(22)=276$, and that the extremal set is unique: for $d\le21$ it is the set of midpoints of the edges of a regular simplex together with its centroid, and for $d=22$ it is the McLaughlin configuration together with its centre. For $10\le d\le22$ every isosceles set with more than $\binom d2+3$ points is a two-distance set or a spherical two-distance set together with its centre, and the number $\binom d2+3$ is attained. The proof combines Blokhuis' decomposition of isosceles sets into mutually orthogonal two-distance sets, for which we give a short self-contained proof (the key step is a special case of Gallai's theorem on edge colourings without rainbow triangles), with the recent determination of the maximum two-distance sets in $\mathbb{R}^d$ for $9\le d\le22$ and with the known maximum sizes of spherical two-distance sets. In dimension nine this gives only $\mathrm{iso}(9)\le47$, and the exact value $46$ requires an additional argument about eight-dimensional two-distance sets with at least $44$ points. We also show that $\mathrm{iso}(23)=\max\{g(23),278\}$, where $g(23)$ is the largest size of a two-distance set in $\mathbb{R}^{23}$, so that $278\le\mathrm{iso}(23)\le575$.

## Logarithmically Slim Polygons

Panagiotis Tselekidis

若无界测地空间的所有测地多边形共享一个细薄常数，作者证明其渐近Assouad–Nagata维数为1。对有界度图，在明确的、足够强的log n级n边形细薄上界下仍得维数1。

We study the relationship between the large-scale geometry of a geodesic metric space and the slimness of its geodesic polygons. We show that if there is a uniform constant $\delta$ such that every geodesic polygon in an unbounded geodesic metric space is $\delta$-slim, then the space has asymptotic Assouad--Nagata dimension one. We further consider graphs of bounded valency and allow the slimness constant of an $n$-gon to grow logarithmically with $n$. More precisely, under an explicit logarithmic bound on the slimness of geodesic $n$-gons, we prove that the graph has asymptotic Assouad--Nagata dimension one. Thus, sufficiently strong logarithmic control on the slimness of geodesic polygons forces one-dimensional large-scale geometry. We also obtain two criteria for graphs, outside the hyperbolic setting, to have asymptotic Assouad--Nagata dimension one. In particular, these criteria show that one-dimensional large-scale geometry can be detected from quantitative restrictions on the slimness of geodesic polygons, without requiring the space to be hyperbolic.

## Filling Lipschitz spheres in metric spaces

Noa Vikman、Stefan Wenger

对具有有界bicombing的度量空间，作者证明单位n球面的Lipschitz映射满足同伦型欧氏等周不等式，适用例子包括CAT(0)空间及赋范线性空间。

We prove a homotopical Euclidean isoperimetric inequality for Lipschitz maps from the unit n-sphere into metric spaces admitting a bounded bicombing, which includes CAT(0)-spaces and normed vector spaces. To do so, we first refine the second-named author's Euclidean isoperimetric inequality for singular Lipschitz chains to this setting. Then, we show that a singular Lipschitz chain filling induces a Lipschitz map filling of almost the same volume or less, by adapting a strategy seen in work of Gromov and others.

## On Diameter Graphs Problems: A proof of Brass's Conjecture

Gyivan Lopez-Campos

对R³中有限点集，以达到集合直径的点对连边。作者证明删去至多一个点即可使直径图三着色，从而回答Brass关于有限删点数的猜想。

The diameter graph of a finite set $S\subset\mathbb{R}^d$ has vertex set $S$ and two points are adjacent when their distance equals $\operatorname{diam} S$. In this paper we study the following question: given $k$ and $d$, is there a constant $c=c(k,d)$ such that from every finite point set $S\subset\mathbb{R}^d$ one can remove at most $c$ points so that the subgraph of the diameter graph induced by the remaining points is $k$-colorable? Brass conjectured that such a constant exists for $d=3$ and $k=3$. We prove this conjecture by showing that at most one point needs to be removed. We also answer a question of Dol'nikov, showing that for every positive integer $m$, there exist a finite point set $S\subset\mathbb{R}^3$ in which every subset of smaller diameter has less than $(|S|-m)/2$ points.

## Assouad--Nagata dimension of systolic complexes with almost flat intervals

Martín Blufstein、Victor Chepoi、Huaitao Gui、Damian Osajda

引入带近乎平坦区间的systolic复形，作为fit systolic复形的子类，证明其Assouad–Nagata维数有普适常数上界。

We introduce the notion of systolic complexes with almost flat intervals. They constitute a subclass of fit systolic complexes and arise naturally in the study of graphical C(3)--T(6) small cancellation complexes. We prove that their Assouad--Nagata dimension is bounded above by a universal constant. As consequences, we obtain universal bounds on the Assouad--Nagata dimension of tame simply connected graphical C(3)--T(6) small cancellation complexes and of finitely generated groups defined by graphical C(3)--T(6) small cancellation presentations. In the appendix, we observe that the proof of finiteness of asymptotic dimension for buildings due to Dymara and Schick also yields finiteness of their asymptotic Assouad--Nagata dimension.

## Non-Pseudo-Arithmetic Hyperbolic 4-Manifolds

Alexander Kolpakov

研究Ma–Zheng三个四维有限体积Coxeter多胞体P₁₁,₆、P₁₁,₇、P₁₁,₈的反射格。Gram矩阵的循环积恢复√5和满足指定二次方程的u，其共轭下判别式为负，故伴随迹域有复嵌入。

We give three explicit finite-volume hyperbolic Coxeter lattices in dimension four whose adjoint trace fields are not totally real. They are the reflection groups of the Ma--Zheng polytopes $P_{11,6}$, $P_{11,7}$, $P_{11,8}$. The obstruction is contained in two cyclic products of the doubled unit-normal Gram matrix $A=2G$. Those products recover elements $s=\sqrt5$ and $u$ satisfying $$ (s-1)u^2+2(s-3)u+s-7=0. $$ After the embedding $s\mapsto-\sqrt5$, this quadratic has discriminant $8(1-\sqrt5)<0$. The cyclic-product field therefore has a complex place. Vinberg's identification of that field with the adjoint trace field and the Emery--Mila total-reality obstruction imply that the three lattices are not pseudo-arithmetic. Torsion-free orientation-preserving finite-index subgroups yield orientable noncompact finite-volume hyperbolic $4$-manifolds with the same obstruction. Exact computations check the census data and provide independent field and finite-volume certificates. A pinned Lean~4 development verifies the radical identity and the number-field obstruction. It also checks the lattice and manifold implications conditional on explicit geometric and literature hypotheses.

## A sharp Bernstein theorem for affine maximal type surfaces

Yalin Sun、Cheng Xing、Ruiwei Xu

在 Calabi 度量完备假设下研究仿射极大型 Monge–Ampère 方程的 Bernstein 刚性。

We investigate Bernstein theorem for the affine maximal type equation \begin{equation*} \sum_{i,j=1}^nf^{ij}(D^a)_{ij}=0,\qquad D=\det(f_{ij}),\qquad a\neq0, \end{equation*} under completeness of the Calabi metric. In dimension two, we prove that every smooth strictly convex solution on a convex domain is a quadratic polynomial whenever $a\notin[-\tfrac23,-\tfrac13]$, resolving a conjecture of Sun and Xu [25]. For every $n\geq2$, we also construct explicit non-quadratic Calabi complete solutions on convex domains for all $a\in[-\tfrac n{n+1},-\tfrac1{n+1}]$. These examples show that the rigidity range is sharp in dimension two.

This paper introduces a class of non-homogeneous self-similar constructions, called $\omega $-substitution graph system, in which the interface vertices are determined by an infinite sequence $\omega .$ The associated graph sequences naturally form compact metric measure spaces, and under suitable assumptions on $\omega$, we establish their convergence in the Gromov-Hausdorff-Prokhorov sense. ## Iterated minimal Darboux transformations

Sakuma Takeshita、Masashi Yasumoto

对欧氏三维空间中的两次极小 Darboux 变换，作者利用等温曲面变换的四维可交换图，在两个参数互异且非零时构造共同极小变换并给出其 Gauss 映射公式。在原曲面的正则无脐点上，两次变换的 Gauss 值相同且不同于原值时产生嵌入平面端，三个值全同则产生重数3的端。悬链面的适当参数给出完备有限全曲率曲面，其平面端数及重数可由参数和初值确定；含重数3端的族中，余下初值参数在旋转意义下实现 López–Ros 形变。

In this paper, we investigate the superposition of two minimal Darboux transformations in Euclidean three-space. A four-dimensional permutability diagram for transformations of isothermic surfaces yields the common minimal transform for distinct nonzero parameters and an explicit formula for its Gauss map. This formula determines the planar ends over regular umbilic-free points of the seed: an end is embedded when the two transformed Gauss maps coincide away from the seed value and has multiplicity 3 when all three values coincide. We illustrate this criterion with the Enneper surface and the catenoid. For the catenoid, suitable parameters give complete surfaces of finite total curvature, and we determine the numbers and multiplicities of their planar ends from the parameters and initial values. In the family with multiplicity-3 ends, the remaining initial-value parameter realizes, up to rotation, the López--Ros deformation.

## Hadamard configurations and sharp cubic-period bounds for stealth in dimensions 10, 11, and 12

Josiah Park

作者在10、11、12维构造非晶格周期点配置，隐匿中心密度为1/24，单位密度隐匿半径为2·24^(−1/n)。数值超过当前纪录晶格堆积之对偶所给配置。对标记周期为2Z^n、任意等权且未必二元的基元，原始隐匿半径至少1时证明尖锐的2n点下界，并以反足 Hadamard 配置刻画等号。进一步得到有界复杂度最优性、这三维数下二元基元类中的尖锐最优性及同时改变基元与标记周期形状的局部极大性。

We construct explicit, nonlattice periodic point configurations in dimensions $10$, $11$ and $12$ with stealth center density $1/24$ and unit-density stealth radius $2\cdot24^{-1/n}$. These values exceed those of duals of the current record lattice packings, without claiming to exceed all possible lattices. The construction uses the $24$ sign rows of an order-$12$ Hadamard matrix and their antipodes, projected onto $n$ coordinates. For an arbitrary, not necessarily binary, equally weighted motif with marked period $2\mathbb{Z}^n$, we prove a sharp $2n$-point lower bound for a raw stealth radius at least one and classify equality by antipodal Hadamard configurations. We deduce bounded-complexity optimality, sharp optimality among binary motifs in dimensions $10$--$12$, and local maximality under simultaneous perturbations of the motif and the marked period shape. A divisibility argument excludes ordinary equally weighted periodic formal duals. Statements are confined to their indicated classes; no global optimality among arbitrary-period configurations is asserted.
