## New type of bubbling solutions to a critical fractional Schrödinger equation with double potentials

Ting Li、Zhongwei Tang、Heming Wang、Xiaojing Zhang

定理1.1及1.2对满足(1.5)的n≥4及分数阶s，在R³×Rⁿ⁻³的径向双势临界方程中，为每个充分大的k构造上下各k个气泡的正解。V、K有界非负，V在中心附近为C²、K为C³，且r₀>0、K与V在中心取正值、∇K的局部度非零。

In this paper, we study the following critical fractional Schrödinger equation: \begin{equation} (-\Delta)^s u+V(|y'|,y'')u=K(|y'|,y'')u^{\frac{n+2s}{n-2s}},\quad u>0,\quad y =(y',y'') \in \mathbb{R}^3\times\mathbb{R}^{n-3}, \qquad(0.1)\end{equation} where $n\geq 3$, $s\in(0,1)$, $V(|y'|,y'')$ and $K(|y'|,y'')$ are two bounded nonnegative potential functions. Under the conditions that $K(r,y'')$ has a stable critical point $(r_0,y_0'')$ with $r_0>0$, $K(r_0,y_0'')>0$ and $V(r_0,y_0'')>0$, we prove that equation (0.1) has a new type of infinitely many solutions that concentrate at points lying on the top and the bottom of a cylinder. In particular, the bubble solutions can concentrate at a pair of symmetric points with respect to the origin. Our proofs make use of a modified finite-dimensional reduction method and local Pohozaev identities.

## Summing over Seifert Manifolds in Narain Ensemble Averages

Kei Kaneda、Masahito Yamazaki

从穿孔曲面乘圆的固定核心出发，沿边界环面粘合实心环面，将 Narain 理论系综平均的 Eisenstein 展开由透镜空间扩至 Seifert 流形的带标记表示。

The ensemble average of Narain conformal field theories over their moduli space is a real-analytic Eisenstein series, whose Poincaré expansion can be interpreted as a sum over lens spaces weighted by the partition functions of Abelian Chern-Simons theory. In this paper we extend this correspondence from lens spaces to Seifert manifolds, which gives an example of a restricted sum over topologies of the kind that arises in three-dimensional quantum gravity. A Seifert manifold is obtained from a fixed core, a punctured surface times a circle, by gluing solid tori to its boundary tori, and we place a copy of the Narain theory on each of these tori. The ensemble average supplies the sum over the solid tori, or more precisely over marked presentations with fixed base genus, number of fibers and integer Seifert invariant. The resulting sum converges absolutely and is a polynomial in the components of the vector-valued Eisenstein series. When all the boundary tori share a single modulus, the average acquires connected corrections, which we identify with wormholes gluing the core to itself; for two boundary tori, the terms with rational Lagrangian sublattices of the Siegel-Eisenstein series are realized by wormholes containing an exceptional fiber. After reduction along the fiber, the construction closely parallels the two-dimensional topological gravity of Marolf and Maxfield, with the base of the fibration playing the role of their spacetime. We also discuss Dehn fillings of 3-manifolds with torus boundary, non-orientable bases, and the surgery formula. Our results suggest that modularity could be an organizing principle in the summation of geometries in quantum gravity.

We demonstrate its potential for interpolation and extrapolation on controlled cross-sectional datasets.

## New lower bounds for kissing numbers in dimensions 32-35 and 37-39

Qihong Lin

将 32、33、34、35、37、38、39 维的接吻数下界依次提高为 352320、370240、390980、415308、499816、605322、768916。

We show that the kissing numbers in dimensions 32, 33, 34, 35, 37, 38 and 39 are at least 352320, 370240, 390980, 415308, 499816, 605322 and 768916, improving the previous bounds 347584, 364736, 384836, 409676, 498024, 591900 and 763668. Except in dimension 38, the configurations are those of Edel, Rains and Sloane with new binary constant-weight codes of weight 8 and minimum distance 8 in the middle layer; each additional word adds 128 points. The codes and lines were found by local search, and all configurations were checked pair by pair in exact integer arithmetic.

## The nonexistence of nine-point crescent configurations

Yifei Wang

对平面九点、无三点共线且无四点共圆，并要求不同距离分别出现 1 至 8 次的构型，作者以精确代数计算证明不存在。

A planar crescent configuration consists of $n$ points, no three collinear and no four concyclic, whose distinct distances have multiplicities $1,2,\ldots,n-1$. We prove by exact computation that no such configuration exists for $n=9$. The three pairs carrying the two least frequent distances yield a six-point subset with at most six distances. Rational linear algebra on quartic polynomials separates the possible six-point distance patterns into those with certified finite lists of normalized distance tuples and a residual family. Compatibility of overlapping subsets forces at least one core into the first family, without classifying the residual realizations. The remaining three points are then obtained by rational constructions over the field of the squared core distances. The multiplication matrices used to enumerate the core distances have order at most twelve. Exhaustive label assignments and exact algebraic comparisons exclude all remaining extensions.

## Grid triples, link cobordisms in $\CPbar$ and the computation of Heegaard-Floer TQFT

Roohallah Mahkam

对大小n的带标记三重网格和分裂复结构，定理1.1/4.2把每个有贡献的指数零类分解成n张欧氏三角薄片。给定相容提升Aᵢ+λᵢT，命题4.4要求每对差三角形Aᵢ−Aⱼ+(λᵢ−λⱼ)T避开(nℤ)²；通过该测试的类依引理4.3恰有一个正则代表，模2计数为1。

We give a combinatorial description of the Heegaard--Floer link-cobordism maps associated with pointed triple grid diagrams in the complement of three $4$-balls in complex projective plane with reverse orientation. Every contributing index-zero holomorphic triangle decomposes into Euclidean triangular sheets. We characterize the compatible collections of sheets that contribute by an explicit geometric separation criterion, expressed as a lattice-point test, and prove that each has mod-\(2\) count one. Individual sheets may wrap around the torus in the unblocked theory, whereas they are embedded in the blocked theory. In each fixed \(\SpinC\) structure, the map is determined by a finite enumeration. A difference number computed from the triangular regions determines the \(\SpinC\) structure of a Whitney triangle. Together with the topology of the associated surface, this gives an explicit formula for the absolute bigrading of each summand of the cobordism map. After basepoint reduction, the formula determines the Laurent monomial representing any nonzero map on the rank-one torsion-free two-variable knot Floer modules, yielding vanishing and ideal-containment criteria. We apply the construction to an affine family of grid links \(K(n;p,s)\), obtaining explicit cobordisms involving infinite families of torus knots and links, together with closed formulas for the bigradings of their distinguished grid states.

## Uniqueness of scalar-flat ALE Kähler metrics

Qi Yao

研究同一 Kähler 类中标量平坦 ALE Kähler 度量在固定欧氏无穷远模型下的唯一性，并讨论固定 Ricci 平坦锥的 AC 情形。

We study uniqueness of scalar-flat Kähler metrics in a fixed Kähler class on an asymptotically locally Euclidean (ALE) Kähler manifold, assuming only differentiated $o(1)$ decay to a fixed Euclidean metric on the end. In complex dimension $n\geq2$, under suitable polynomial decay of the reference metric, we prove that the relative Mabuchi functional is finite, continuous, and convex along weak geodesics whose endpoint potentials satisfy $u=O(r^{-\gamma})$, $\gamma>2n-4$, together with the corresponding derivative estimates. Combining this convexity with the analysis of the Lichnerowicz operator in weighted spaces, we obtain uniqueness by a perturbation argument. Decay improvement and comparison of the asymptotic charts produce a global potential with the required decay for the difference of the two cohomologous scalar-flat Kähler forms. The argument also extends to asymptotically conical Kähler metrics approaching a fixed Ricci-flat Kähler cone under prescribed decay assumptions.

## Conjugate radius and parabolicity along the Ricci flow

Chandan Kumar Mondal

研究完备非紧 Ricci 流上的抛物性及共轭半径。

We study the behaviour of the conjugate radius under the Ricci flow on complete noncompact manifolds. We show that if $(M^n,g(t))_{t\in[0,T]}$ is a complete Ricci flow with nonnegative scalar curvature at $t=0$, the initial metric is parabolic and not Ricci-flat, and the Einstein tensor $\mathrm{Ric}-\tfrac12 Rg$ is bounded above, then parabolicity is preserved along the flow and the conjugate radius of $g(t)$ is finite for every $t\in(0,T]$. As an application, we prove that a complete noncompact gradient shrinking Ricci soliton whose volume growth is subquadratic, in the sense that $\liminf_{r\to\infty}\Vol(B(p,r))/r^2=0$, has finite conjugate radius.

## A surface-group action on real-hyperbolic space with no Cannon--Thurston map

Bruno Dular

定理1.1给亏格431闭定向曲面群在某有限维实双曲空间的离散忠实纯loxodromic表示，非平凡元平移长度有统一正下界。证明构造的hₙd⁺在源边界趋于b⁺，目标中的端点像趋于ρ(b)⁻，从而排除连续Cannon–Thurston延拓。同一环境还给出秩287自由群反例。

We present discrete, faithful and purely loxodromic representations of a closed surface group of genus 431, and of a free group, into the group of isometries of a finite-dimensional real-hyperbolic space for which no Cannon--Thurston map exists.
