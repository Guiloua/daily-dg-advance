## Simply connected quotients of fake quadrics

Zoltán Szabó

介绍假二次曲面的算术构造，并研究全纯及反全纯商的基本群。作者以全实三次域上的四元数代数给显式格和对合。

Fake quadrics are minimal complex surfaces of general type with $b_1=0$ and $e=4$. The paper gives a short introduction to their arithmetic construction, aimed at low-dimensional topologists, and studies their holomorphic and antiholomorphic quotients and their fundamental groups. The running example in the paper comes from a quaternion algebra over a totally real cubic field. The lattice is generated by three explicit pairs of matrices. A holomorphic involution gives (after resolving the singularities) a simply connected minimal complex surface of general type with $p_g=0$ and $K^2=4$, a smooth $4$-manifold homeomorphic but non-diffeomorphic to $CP^2$ blown up $5$ times. An antiholomorphic involution gives a smooth four-manifold homeomorphic to $S^4$, with a knotted torus as branch locus.

## Local Aronson-Bénilan estimates for the porous medium and fast diffusion equations on collapsing manifolds

Jinpeng Miao、Bin Shen

在完备非紧流形上，以Ricci负部尺度不变Lp小性、p>n/2，给正多孔介质及快扩散解的局部Aronson–Bénilan估计，允许单位球体积塌缩。

We establish local Aronson-Bénilan estimates for positive solutions to the porous medium and fast diffusion equations on complete noncompact Riemannian manifolds with a small scale-invariant integral bound on the negative part of the Ricci curvature in $L^p$, where $p>n/2$. No uniform lower bound on the volume of unit balls is assumed, and hence the results apply to manifolds that may collapse. For the fast diffusion equation, we obtain the estimate in the range $1-2/n<m<1$. The auxiliary function method, which was designed for the heat equation under integral curvature conditions, is not directly applicable to those two nonlinear equations. The pressure operator in both cases contains a solution-dependent coefficient, which produces a residual term involving $\partial_t J$ in the evolution inequality. Its treatment requires a positive lower bound for $J$, and moreover, control of $-\partial_t\log J$. We obtain these bounds by linearizing the auxiliary equation, applying Duhamel's formula, and combining local Dirichlet heat kernel estimates with a singular Volterra inequality. Additionally, in the fast diffusion case, the negativity of $v$ changes the sign structure of the evolution inequality, so a separate coercive estimate is required to close the argument. Local Aronson-Bénilan estimates follow for both diffusion regimes and integrating them along space-time curves gives the corresponding Harnack inequalities.

## Strong unique continuation on Riemannian manifolds for degenerate elliptic operators with convexified weights

Bin Shen、Zisu Zhao

研究权重在流形内部一点p消失的退化椭圆方程。作者用凸化径向权重处理曲率引入的几何误差，证明在p无限阶消失的弱解在连通域恒零。

In this manuscript, we consider a degenerate elliptic equation on a Riemannian manifold whose weight vanishes at an interior point $p$, and prove the strong unique continuation property (SUCP) at $p$. The curved setting gives rise to intrinsic geometric error terms that obstruct the analytic estimates available in the Euclidean case. To overcome this, we introduce a convexified radial weight that restores coercivity in the Carleman commutator. Combining the resulting Carleman estimate with a Hardy-type inequality and a Caccioppoli inequality, we show that any weak solution vanishing to infinite order at the degeneracy point must be identically zero on the connected domain. This result is the first SUCP theorem for this class of degenerate operators on general Riemannian manifolds.

## Parabolic frequency and backward uniqueness on compact Finsler metric measure spaces

Bin Shen、Zisu Zhao

在闭Finsler度量测度空间上建立固定或演化背景的抛物频率单调公式。耦合流中用局部径向屏障构造线性化共轭热方程的正全局上、下解。

We establish monotonicity formulas for parabolic frequency functions on closed Finsler metric measure spaces with fixed or evolving metrics and measures. When the reference measure is fixed, an integral bound on the metric variation yields monotonicity for the Finsler heat equation. For coupled metric measure flows, we construct positive global supersolutions and subsolutions of a linearized conjugate heat equation from local radial barriers. These weights define a frequency function whose monotonicity follows from an integral bound involving the metric variation and the change in weighted Ricci curvature. For parabolic equations with a source controlled by the solution and its gradient, we obtain a differential inequality for the frequency and prove that a solution vanishing at the terminal time must vanish throughout the preceding interval. The proof controls the comparison of the two weights through an energy quotient computed with the subsolution measure.

## Coefficient Riemann--Hilbert for an Affine Singular Foliation

Qingyun Zeng

对叶状结构〈x∂x,y∂x〉，作者在R²每个普通开集上证明光滑系数积分给严格保单位的A∞拟等价，允许内部上同调秩跳跃。

For the affine foliation $\langle x\partial_x,y\partial_x\rangle$, we prove that smooth coefficient integration is a strictly unital $A_\infty$ quasi-equivalence on every ordinary open subset of $\mathbb R^2$, for globally bounded, degreewise finite-projective real coefficient bodies, allowing internal cohomology of nonconstant rank. A horizontal-hull restriction of a finite Lie $2$-groupoid model admits a smooth simplicial section of the comparison from full Maurer--Cartan integration, with a homotopy relative to all unit-generated simplices. Infinitely flat and nongeneric rank-jumping examples illustrate the nonregular theorem. Nonzero residue obstructs pullback through any strict map to an ordinary groupoid nerve with represented objects in the stated raw coefficient theory. For regular vertical families over $S\times\mathbb R^2$, we also prove a finite-projective bundle-valued theorem natural under smooth parameter pullback; its two-term invariants are bundle arrows, rather than pointwise rank profiles.

## Cobordism maps and Atiyah-Floer isomorphisms for framed instanton homology I

Bernd Johannes Wuebben

作者证明带标架瞬子同调与Heegaard Lagrangian Floer同调的Atiyah–Floer型同构，在模2系数下交织配边映射，辛侧只计全纯曲线。

We prove that isomorphisms of the type constructed by Daemi, Fukaya and Lipyanskiy, between framed instanton homology and the Lagrangian Floer homology of a Heegaard splitting, intertwine the cobordism maps of Kronheimer and Mrowka with maps defined by holomorphic curves alone, over the field of two elements. This holds for every connected cobordism between connected three-manifolds carrying a framed arc between its ends and an $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ bundle trivialized along the ends and the arc. The symplectic maps count holomorphic triangles in moduli spaces of flat connections on surfaces, with the correspondence of a compression body as boundary condition for one- and three-handles. Consequently these maps are independent of the handle decomposition and of the Heegaard data, and form a functor isomorphic to framed instanton homology; where both are defined, our isomorphisms agree with those of Daemi, Fukaya and Lipyanskiy modulo two. The proof rests on two analytic results for the mixed anti-self-duality and holomorphic curve equation: an adiabatic limit in which a compression body collapses next to a holomorphic strip and becomes the Lagrangian boundary condition of its flat connections, and an excision theorem along a torus.

## Classification of real Lie algebras of dimension six

Mehdi Nadjafikhah

用Levi–Malcev分解、幂零根基约束与中心扩张整理六维实Lie代数同构分类，并给矩阵代表和确定性识别算法。

We summarize a coordinate-free classification of six-dimensional real Lie algebras up to isomorphism. Instead of solving the overdetermined Jacobi system of 120 quadratic equations on 90 structure constants modulo $\mathrm{GL}(6,\mathbb{R})$, the method uses the Levi--Malcev decomposition, Mostow's nilradical constraints, and the Skjelbred--Sund cohomological central extension technique. In the semisimple sector, the four real forms of $A_1\oplus A_1$ are classified by Killing signature invariants, with explicit matrix representatives for $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{C})_{\mathbb{R}}\cong\mathfrak{so}(3,1)$. For non-solvable algebras with three-dimensional radical, representation-theoretic rigidity shows that $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ cannot couple indecomposably with non-abelian radicals, yielding exactly three indecomposable classes governed by $\mathfrak{so}(3)$ and $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R})$. In the solvable category, Mostow's theorem restricts the nilradical dimension to $\{3,4,5,6\}$. The nilpotent sector in dimension six has twenty-four indecomposable isomorphism classes plus decomposable counterparts. Solvable non-nilpotent algebras are classified by nilradical codimension, with explicit real Jordan matrix representations for codimension one and two that resolve continuous moduli under gauge actions. The work also computes the minimal faithful representation dimension $\mu(\mathfrak{g})$ under Ado's theorem, relates the classification to invariant complex structures on nilmanifolds, and provides a deterministic, page-breakable identification algorithm.
