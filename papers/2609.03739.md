# Rigidity and volume pinching for the sharp gradient estimate in positive Ricci curvature

- **作者：** Junyoung Kim、Jiewon Park
- **arXiv：** [2609\.03739](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03739) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.03739)
- **分类：** math\.DG、math\.AP
- **进展类型：** 推广/加强
- **阅读优先级：** 92/100 · 高优先级
- **分析深度：** 已补读正文关键部分

**完成的工作**

在正 Ricci 曲率下证明 Green 函数距离型函数尖锐梯度估计的定量几乎刚性：平均梯度亏损控制体积亏损，并推出 Einstein 流形间隙和四维闭 Einstein 刚性。

**使用技术**

- Green 函数与距离型函数
- 积分梯度亏损
- 体积比较与几乎刚性
- 新的单调量公式

**可能的突破**

把尖锐点态梯度估计提升为可量化的体积接近结论，并以新单调公式得到四维 Einstein 刚性。

**限制与不确定性**

需要 Ric≥\(n−1\)g，四维刚性另依赖 Einstein 条件；亏损到更强度量收敛的定量尚未在摘要中给出。

**排序理由**

与 Ricci 曲率、体积刚性和比较几何核心方向高度相关，且提供可复用的亏损—体积机制。

**原始英文摘要**

Colding established a sharp gradient estimate for the Green function on manifolds with nonnegative Ricci curvature, which was extended to positive Ricci curvature by Manea recently\. We prove almost rigidity of such gradient estimates for positive Ricci curvature\. We show that the average of the gradient deficit, namely $\fint (1-|\nabla b|^2)\,dV$, controls the volume deficit in a quantitative manner; here $b$ is the distance-like function defined using the Green function\. This implies a quantitative almost rigidity theorem for manifolds with $\mathrm{Ric}\ge(n-1)g$ and a gap theorem for Einstein manifolds\. We also prove a rigidity theorem for closed 4-dimensional Einstein manifolds by finding a new monotonicity formula\.
