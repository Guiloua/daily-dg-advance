# Sophus Lie's problem on two-dimensional metrics with projective symmetries: completing the local classification

- **作者：** Gianni Manno、Vladimir S\. Matveev、Filippo Salis
- **arXiv：** [2609\.05280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05280) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.05280)
- **分类：** math\.DG
- **进展类型：** 新定理/分类
- **阅读优先级：** 80/100 · 高优先级
- **分析深度：** 已补读正文关键部分

**完成的工作**

补齐具有至少二维射影对称代数的二维伪黎曼度量在非正则作用点附近的局部等距分类。

**使用技术**

- 射影 Lie 对称
- 奇异轨道
- 局部正规形
- 伪黎曼等距分类
- 沿模型测地线分析度量不变量
- 正规模型跨奇异边界的拼合与不可延拓性

**可能的突破**

与已知正则分类拼合，完成该限定条件下 Sophus Lie 1882 年问题的局部分类。

**限制与不确定性**

是二维、局部且对称代数维数至少二的分类，不是一般度量的全局分类。 本条基于已检查来源的阅读分析，关键结论请回查原文。

**排序理由**

历史问题的缺失奇异分支得到处理，分类范围明确，适合特殊几何读者优先阅读。

**原始英文摘要**

We complete the local classification, up to isometry, of $2$-dimensional pseudo-Riemannian metrics \(i\.e\. both Riemannian and Lorentzian\), admitting a projective symmetry algebra of dimension at least two\. The new contribution is the treatment of the non-regular case: we obtain a complete list of mutually non-isometric normal forms in neighbourhoods of points where the action of the projective Lie symmetry algebra fails to be regular, including all singular behaviours that can occur\. Together with the known classification in the regular case, this completes the solution of the problem posed by Sophus Lie in 1882\.
