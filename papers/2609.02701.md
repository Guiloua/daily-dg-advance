# Circuit Decomposition for Triangulations of Surfaces

- **作者：** Jens Harlander、Maizie Quatrone
- **arXiv：** [2609\.02701](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02701) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02701)
- **分类：** math\.GT
- **进展类型：** 组合拓扑刻画
- **阅读优先级：** 58/100 · 中优先级
- **分析深度：** 摘要级分析

**完成的工作**

研究闭紧曲面三角剖分的二维回路分解，以路径内角构造第一上同调障碍，并证明其消失等价于可分解性。

**使用技术**

- 二维 Euler 回路
- 第一上同调障碍
- 可定向二重覆盖

**可能的突破**

将回路分解的组合可行性转化为拓扑障碍；非可定向情形归约到可定向二重覆盖。

**限制与不确定性**

对象为曲面三角剖分，不涵盖一般高维单纯复形；摘要未提供算法复杂度保证。

**排序理由**

清楚的组合—拓扑接口，适合相关离散课题；与曲率和几何分析主线距离较远。

**原始英文摘要**

An Euler circuit of a graph is a closed path that visits every edge of the graph exactly once\. Euler circuit and circuit decomposition problems can also be formulated for higher dimensional simplicial complexes\. An Euler k-circuit in K is a cyclic sequence of vertices v\_1\.\.\.v\_n such that every k\+1 adjacent terms \{ v\_i,v\_\{i\+1\},\.\.\.,v\_\{i\+k\} \} \(indexed modulo n\) form a k-simplex, and every k-simplex of K appears exactly once in the sequence v\_1v\_2\.\.\.v\_n\(v\_1v\_2\.\.\.v\_k\)\. We investigate the 2-circuit decomposition problem for triangulated closed compact surfaces\. For an orientable triangulated surface we use interior angles of paths to define an obstruction that lives in the first cohomology of the surface\. It vanishes if and only if the surface has a 2-circuit decomposition\. We also show that a non-orientable triangulated surface has a 2-circuit decomposition if and only if its orientable 2-fold cover does\.
