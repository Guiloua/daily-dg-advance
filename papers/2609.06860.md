# Bridge numbers of virtual graphs and handlebody-links

- **作者：** Nikolaos Chantis、Puttipong Pongtanapaisan
- **arXiv：** [2609\.06860](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06860) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.06860)
- **分类：** math\.GT
- **进展类型：** 新方法/构造
- **阅读优先级：** 53/100 · 中优先级
- **分析深度：** 摘要级分析

**完成的工作**

构造虚图、ravel 及几乎无结图的桥指标差无界族，并计算若干精确桥指标。

**使用技术**

- biquandle 染色
- 固定流
- 边连通和

**可能的突破**

分离图桥指标与正则邻域桥指标。

**限制与不确定性**

不同桥指标定义不可混用，结论针对所构造族。 研究结论按摘要级分析；未完成正文方法核验。

**排序理由**

低维拓扑专门不变量构造，主线联系较弱。

**原始英文摘要**

We study bridge indices of graphs, virtual graphs, and their handlebody classes\. We construct virtual ravels for which the difference between bridge index and weighted overpass bridge index is unbounded\. The lower bound uses exponentially many colorings for one fixed flow in a $\Z_2$-family of biquandles\. For each negative Euler characteristic we construct classical almost unknotted graphs of one fixed trivalent graph with arbitrarily large bridge index\. We also obtain unbounded even bridge indices for ravels of each fixed bouquet rank\. A separate almost unknotted family has an unbounded difference between its spatial-graph bridge index and the bridge index of its regular neighborhood\. Finally, edge connected sums of Suzuki curves give exact constrained and unconstrained bridge indices whose difference is unbounded\.
