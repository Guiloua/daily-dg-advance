# Solutions to fourth order degenerate parabolic equations obtained via weighted energy dissipation

- **作者：** Daniel Matthes、Vera Pazukhina
- **arXiv：** [2609\.05293](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05293) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.05293)
- **分类：** math\.AP、math\.MG
- **进展类型：** 新方法/构造
- **阅读优先级：** 77/100 · 高优先级
- **分析深度：** 已补读正文关键部分

**完成的工作**

用加权能量耗散变分法构造四阶退化 Cahn–Hilliard 型方程的全时间近似，通过时间椭圆正则化保持相场约束。

**使用技术**

- WED 变分法
- 度量梯度流
- 时间椭圆正则化
- Lyapunov 估计

**可能的突破**

把 WED 从常见 Hilbert 或二阶 Wasserstein 场景扩展到更高阶的度量梯度流。

**限制与不确定性**

正文具体在一维环面上讨论线性薄膜迁移率及 Lipschitz 一致凹的 Cahn–Hilliard 迁移率；不是任意维数四阶方程的一般理论。

**排序理由**

度量变分技术可复用，并为高阶退化 PDE 提供区别于空间正则化的路线。

**原始英文摘要**

We apply the variational method of Weighted Energy Dissipation \(WED\) to obtain a global-in-time approximation of solutions to fourth order degenerate parabolic equations of Cahn-Hilliard type\. Differently from the standard approach to the existence theory, WED induces an elliptic regularization in time, not in space\. The confinement on the phase field is guaranteed already for the approximation, a priori estimates follow without modification of Lyapunov functionals, and the approximations are weakly differentiable in time --- even twice in the case of linear mobility\. While WED has been widely used for the construction of gradient flows in Hilbert spaces, this appears to be the first application of WED to a metric gradient flow beyond second order PDEs of Wasserstein type\.
