**完成的工作**

构造复维至少五时 balanced 双曲性不开放的一参数形变族，同时建立 Kähler 与 balanced 双曲性的若干稳定性准则。

**使用技术**

- 移动纯型上同调交
- Aeppli 与 Dolbeault 类
- hard Lefschetz
- 万有覆叠有界原始形式

**可能的突破**

用反例划定稳定性边界，并把有界势到有界原始形式的提升障碍压缩为一个有界 ∂ 方程及商空间障碍。

**限制与不确定性**

反例的邻近纤维甚至不再 balanced；在邻近纤维保持 balanced 的附加条件下，双曲性是否持续仍被列为开放问题。正面结论需要上同调、秩或横截条件。

**排序理由**

反例与稳定机制互补，连接覆叠空间分析和复几何形变。

**AI 协作披露**

反例由 gpt-5\.6-sol 辅助构造，作者随后仔细核验其数学正确性。

**原始英文摘要**

We study the deformation stability of Kähler and balanced hyperbolicity\. Balanced hyperbolicity is not open in general: in every complex dimension $N\\geq5$ we construct a one-parameter family with balanced hyperbolic central fibre and non-balanced nearby fibres\. For positive results, we develop three complementary mechanisms\. A finite-dimensional moving-intersection framework tracks $\\widetilde d$-bounded de Rham classes through moving pure-type loci; its Aeppli and Dolbeault realizations yield continuation, transversality, and positivity criteria for balanced and Kähler hyperbolicity\. A topological mechanism combines the graded-ideal property of hyperbolic cohomology with the hard Lefschetz theorem to obtain saturation and higher-power propagation results\. Finally, on the universal cover, we reduce the passage from a bounded $\(\\partial\+\\bar\\partial\)$-potential to a bounded $d$-primitive to a single bounded top-row $\\partial$-equation, and package the dependence on the potential into a canonical quotient obstruction\. Together, these viewpoints yield a range of deformation stability results\.
