# On the Finiteness of Anosov flows on $3$-manifolds via Contact Geometry

- **作者：** Jonathan Bowden、Vincent Colin
- **arXiv：** [2609\.03700](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03700) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.03700)
- **分类：** math\.DS、math\.GT、math\.SG

**完成的工作**

证明与 Anosov 流关联的一对反向定向接触结构的同伦类决定流的同伦轨道等价类，从而得到闭双曲三流形上 Anosov 流的有限性及显式上界。

**使用技术**

- 正负接触结构对
- Eliashberg–Thurston/Mitsumatsu 对应
- 普遍紧接触结构有限性
- JSJ 分解

**可能的突破**

把动力系统分类问题转化为接触结构分类，首次给出双曲三流形上按同伦轨道等价的有限性，并扩展到部分 pseudo-Anosov 流。

**限制与不确定性**

有限性按同伦轨道等价而非更强共轭；pseudo-Anosov 推广要求奇异轨道补空间无环面等条件。

**排序理由**

将接触拓扑工具用于 Anosov 流分类，结论清晰且连接低维拓扑与动力系统。

**原始英文摘要**

Following Eliashberg-Thurston and Mitsumatsu, one can associate a transverse pair of oppositely oriented contact structures to any Anosov flow\. We show that the isotopy classes of these contact structures completely determine the flow up to isotopy orbit equivalence\. This then implies that the number of Anosov flows modulo isotopy orbit equivalence on a closed hyperbolic $3$-manifold is finite\. This approach also yields an explicit bound on the number of orbit equivalence classes of Anosov flows in terms of the number of universally tight contact structures\. In addition, we show finiteness of pseudo-Anosov flows for which the complement of the singular orbits is atoroidal and, in the non-hyperbolic case, we obtain a control on the dynamics of Anosov flows on the hyperbolic pieces of the JSJ-decomposition\.
