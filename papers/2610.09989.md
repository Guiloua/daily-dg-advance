# Positive bisectional curvature, Ricci mass, and the fundamental group

Theorem 1.1 对 complete noncompact Kähler、positive holomorphic bisectional curvature 证明 Ricci form 顶次积分有限，Theorem 1.2 因而推出基本群有限。关键是构造 Lipschitz psh function，具有正且有限的 Bedford–Taylor Monge–Ampère mass；以 truncated Bellman operator 作一致椭圆逼近，再用 concave elliptic regularity 与 Błocki 型梯度估计。
