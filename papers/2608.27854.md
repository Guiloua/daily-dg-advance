# Improved $\\ell\_0$-Isoperimetry for Convex Bodies via Mass Transport

- **作者：** Manuel Fernandez
- **arXiv：** [2608\.27854](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27854) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.27854)
- **分类：** math\.FA、cs\.CG、math\.MG、math\.ST
- **进展类型：** 等周下界改进与采样应用
- **阅读优先级：** 79/100 · 高优先级
- **分析深度：** 已补读正文关键部分

**完成的工作**

对夹在两个可能不同中心的无条件凸体 Q 拷贝之间的凸体 K，证明坐标改变意义下的 ℓ0 边界扩张下界，并包含小集合的对数增益；由此改进 Coordinate Hit and Run 的混合时间控制。

**使用技术**

- 质量运输的坐标离散化
- 连续 Hamming 图
- 规范路径与多商品流
- 小集合扩张及导通率

**可能的突破**

直接覆盖任意无条件 Q 的正则性，相比对应 Euclidean/立方体基准下界改进一个 n 因子；把运输结构转化成坐标局部移动的边界控制。

**限制与不确定性**

上下界仍有 n 因子缺口，作者也将进一步改进列为猜想。ℓ0 边界依赖坐标方向，任意仿射变换不保持该量；采样结论需区分初始化和正则性假设。

**排序理由**

质量运输与凸几何方法可复用，并产生定量算法收益；但尚未达最优阶，且坐标依赖限制几何不变性，排在完整结构性解决之后。

**原始英文摘要**

We study $\\ell\_0$ isoperimetry for a convex body $K\\subset \\mathbb\{R\}^n$, $n\\ge2$\. For a Borel set $S\\subset K$, let $\\partial\_0^K S$ be the set of points in $K \\setminus S$ that can be reached from $S$ by changing at most one coordinate \(i\.e\. the $\\ell\_0$ boundary of $S$\)\. Suppose that, for some unconditional convex body $Q \\subset \\mathbb\{R\}^n$, numbers $r,R&gt;0$, and possibly different centers $x\_0,y\_0$, \\\[ x\_0\+rQ \\subset K\\subset y\_0\+RQ\. \\\] Writing $s=\\text\{vol\}\(S\)/\\text\{vol\}\(K\)$, we prove that whenever $0&lt;s \\le 1/2$, \\\[ \\frac\{\\text\{vol\}\(\\partial\_0^K S\)\}\{\\text\{vol\}\(S\)\} \\ge \\frac\{cr\}\{nR\} \\min\\left\\\{1,\\frac\{\\log\(e/s\)\}\{n\}\\right\\\}, \\\] where $c &gt; 0$ is an absolute constant\. Consequently, the associated $\\ell\_0$-isoperimetric coefficient is at least $cr/\(n^2R\)$\. Previous direct lower bounds were only known for $\\ell\_2$ and $\\ell\_\\infty$ regularity whereas our lower bound holds directly for any $Q$-regularity, where $Q$ is an unconditional convex body\. Compared to $\\ell\_2$ and $\\ell\_\\infty$ regularity, our lower bound result improves upon the previously best known lower bounds, for any $s$, by a factor of $n$\. As an application of our result, we give improved mixing time bounds for the Coordinate Hit and Run walk \(CHAR\)\. Our proof of the lower bound is based on a modification of the method of canonical paths applied to a continuous Hamming graph over our convex body\. Our construction of canonical paths can be viewed as a suitable coordinate discretization of certain mass transport maps from $S$ to $S^c$\. We also give complementary upper-bounds for any $Q$-regularity, with an overall factor of $n$ gap between the two\.
