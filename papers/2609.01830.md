# The bounded area class of negatively curved surfaces

- **作者：** Roberto Frigerio、Ervin Hadziosmanovic
- **arXiv：** [2609\.01830](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01830) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.01830)
- **分类：** math\.GT、math\.AG、math\.DG
- **进展类型：** 新刻画
- **阅读优先级：** 72/100 · 中优先级
- **分析深度：** 摘要级分析

**完成的工作**

证明除圆盘与圆柱外，完备夹挤负曲率曲面的面积形式给出非零二阶有界上同调类；并研究该类随度量的变化。

**使用技术**

- 有界上同调
- 负曲率曲面的面积类
- 固定面积下的范数极小化

**可能的突破**

紧曲面中常曲率度量恰为该范数的唯一极小者，而无限型曲面的不同双曲结构也可能无法被此类区分。

**限制与不确定性**

非零性要求完备及夹挤负曲率；识别常曲率的极小化结论限定在紧致、固定面积情形。

**排序理由**

连接曲率与拓扑不变量，且同时给出刚性和识别能力的边界；对曲面与有界上同调方向直接相关。

**原始英文摘要**

Let $S$ be an oriented surface, possibly of infinite type, endowed with a complete Riemannian metric with pinched negative curvature\. We prove that the area form defines a non-trivial class in the second bounded cohomology group of $S$, unless $S$ is diffeomorphic to the disc or the cylinder\. This is in sharp contrast with the $n$-dimensional case, $n&gt;2$, where the non-triviality of the volume form in bounded cohomology is related to the Cheeger constant of the manifold\. We also discuss how the bounded area class depends on the metric: we prove that, for compact surfaces, it recognizes constant curvature metrics among pinched negatively curved ones, while, even in the case of surfaces of infinite type, it does not distinguish non-isometric hyperbolic structures\. More precisely, when $S$ is compact we show that, among the negatively curved structures of fixed area, the ones with constant curvature provide the unique minimizers for the norm of the bounded area class\.
