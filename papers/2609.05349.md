**完成的工作**

证明 Kontorovich–Nakamura 关于组合多面体所关联算术双曲反射群可公度类的猜想，并刻画算术理想直角双曲多面体的种子拼合结构。

**使用技术**

- Koebe–Andreev–Thurston 对应
- 算术反射群
- 可公度
- 多面体拼合
- Scharlau–Walhorn 的 49 类反射格筛除
- 面与顶点障碍及组合论证

**可能的突破**

将所有相关算术理想直角多面体归约到三类种子，从而得到猜想中的可公度分类。

**限制与不确定性**

对象限该构造产生的反射群及算术理想直角多面体，并非所有双曲反射群分类。 本条基于已检查来源的阅读分析，关键结论请回查原文。

**排序理由**

算术与双曲几何的结构分类强，种子分解也有独立几何价值。

**原始英文摘要**

The Koebe-Andreev-Thurston theorem assigns a 3-dimensional hyperbolic reflection group to each combinatorial polyhedron\. A natural question is: which of them are arithmetic? In 2016, Kontorovich-Nakamura conjectured that all arithmetic reflection groups obtained in this way are commensurable to those obtained from the tetrahedron, square pyramid, or cuboctahedron\. In this paper, we prove the conjecture\. It is a consequence of the following result of independent interest: all arithmetic ideal, right-angled hyperbolic polyhedra are obtained by gluing together copies of one of three \`\`seed'' polyhedra\.
