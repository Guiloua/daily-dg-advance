**原始英文摘要**

For $0\le m\le n$, let $c_{m,n}$ be the infimum of scales at which the Vietoris--Rips filtration of the round sphere $\mathbb{S}^m$ admits a continuous odd map from $\mathbb{S}^n$\. A quantitative Borsuk--Ulam theorem gives $c_{m,n}/2\le d_{\mathrm{GH}}(\mathbb{S}^m,\mathbb{S}^n)$, and it was asked whether equality always holds\. Using a synchronized product-measure lift of the spherical join, we construct continuous odd maps between Vietoris--Rips metric thickenings with target scale equal to the maximum input scale\. Iteration gives $c_{m+d,n+d}\le c_{m,n}$ for every integer $d\ge0$\. More generally, the finite join law adds $m_i+1$ and $n_i+1$ separately and bounds the resulting $c$-value by $\max_i c_{m_i,n_i}$\. For $0\\le r&lt;π$, the pairs $(k,\ell)$ with $c_{k-1,\ell-1}\le r$ are closed under addition, so Fekete's lemma gives a limit for the maximal admissible $\ell/k$ as $k\to\infty$\. This structure, exact values, and projective-code estimates give finite and asymptotic bounds\. If $1\\le m\_j&lt;n\_j$, $m_j\to\infty$, and $\log(n_j+1)=o(m_j)$, then $c_{m_j,n_j}\toπ/2$\. At fixed $r\in(π/2,π)$, projective-code and projective-covering estimates give lower and upper exponential bounds for the largest $n$ with $c_{m,n}\le r$\. Together with exact values, these yield a sharp transition: the largest $n$ admitting an odd map is $m$ at scale $π/2$ and grows exponentially with $m$ at each fixed $r\in(π/2,π)$\. We finally return to the conjectural equality\. We introduce a Gromov--Hausdorff-stable invariant from chromatic numbers of Borsuk-graph filtrations\. Combining projective-code upper bounds for $c_{m,n}$ with chromatic lower bounds from spherical-cap volumes, we show that if $m\_j&lt;n\_j$, $m_j\to\infty$, and $n_j/m_j\toλ\ge9$, then $d\_\{\\mathrm\{GH\}\}\(\\mathbb\{S\}^\{m\_j\},\\mathbb\{S\}^\{n\_j\}\)&gt;c\_\{m\_j,n\_j\}/2$ for all sufficiently large $j$, producing infinitely many counterexamples\.
