# Gauge theory and symplectic structures

- **作者：** Partha Ghosh
- **arXiv：** [2609\.02699](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02699) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02699)
- **分类：** math\.DG、math\.SG
- **进展类型：** 新方程/研究框架
- **阅读优先级：** 79/100 · 高优先级
- **分析深度：** 已补读正文关键部分

**完成的工作**

提出高偶数维几乎 Hermitian 流形上的规范椭圆系统，在四维还原带 Taubes 扰动的 Seiberg–Witten 方程；典范 spinC 结构下指标为零。

**使用技术**

- Dirac–曲率耦合系统
- Weitzenböck 公式
- 大扰动横截性

**可能的突破**

构造几乎 Kähler 情形的典范解并证明大参数横截性，为高维辛结构障碍计划提供可操作的方程基础。

**限制与不确定性**

尚未建立所需整体紧性或计数不变量；六维唯一性还要求额外三形式 β=0，不能称已得到高维辛障碍。

**排序理由**

提供新的高维规范论入口，潜在影响大但计划尚未闭合；已核对指标、横截性、条件唯一性及作者列出的障碍。

**原始英文摘要**

Motivated by the problem of finding obstructions to the existence of symplectic structures in dimensions higher than four, we introduce an elliptic system of equations on almost Hermitian manifolds that reduces to the Seiberg--Witten equations with Taubes' perturbation in dimension four\. To define the equations, one needs to choose a spin$^{\mathbb C}$ structure on the manifold\. We prove that the system has index zero for the canonical spin$^{\mathbb C}$ structure\. Moreover, on almost Kähler \(symplectic\) manifolds, we construct a canonical solution and prove its transversality with large values of the perturbation parameter, while in dimension six we obtain uniqueness of the canonical solution under a certain assumption\. We also show that on Kähler manifolds the equations reduce, under a natural ansatz, to the vortex equations\. These results provide evidence toward a possible higher dimensional gauge theory capable of producing invariants of almost complex structures and ultimately, obstructions to compatible symplectic forms\.
