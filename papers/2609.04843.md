We analyze the relation between connection matrices and Berry phases on the Siegel-Jacobi disk $\\mathcal\{D\}^J\_1$ and Siegel-Jacobi upper half-plane $\\mathcal\{X\}^J\_1$ and we explicitly compute the connection matrices and the Berry connections on these homogeneous spaces in different coordinates\.
