# Homogeneous Milnor fibers and Kato--Matsumoto bounds via simplicial multiwedges

- **作者：** Masaharu Ishikawa、Tat-Thang Nguyen
- **arXiv：** [2609\.11396](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11396) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.11396)
- **分类：** math\.AT、math\.AG、math\.CO、math\.GT
- **进展类型：** 新方法/构造
- **阅读优先级：** 70/100 · 中优先级
- **分析深度：** 摘要级分析

**完成的工作**

构造达到 Kato–Matsumoto 连通性界且具有高阶非平凡 Massey 积的齐次 Milnor 纤维。

**使用技术**

- 单纯 multiwedge
- 多面体 nerve
- 加权齐次实现

**可能的突破**

同时控制连通性与非形式性，回应 Suciu 的两个问题。

**限制与不确定性**

参数 n≥3、s≥2，精确连通性与有理上同调操作不可混同。 当前为摘要级分析，方法及结论须回查原文。

**排序理由**

奇点与同伦工具结合，构造可供拓扑方向复用。

**原始英文摘要**

For every $n\geq 3$ and $s\geq 2$, we construct a homogeneous polynomial of degree $n(n+1)/2$ whose Milnor fiber is exactly $2s$-connected and whose rational cohomology contains a strictly defined nontrivial $n$-fold Massey product on classes of degree $2s+1$, implying that the Milnor fiber is non-formal, while attaining the Kato--Matsumoto connectivity bound\. Our construction is based on the simplicial multiwedges of the nerve complexes of simple polytopes introduced by Limonchenko, combined with Suciu's realization of weighted homogeneous Milnor fibers\. We thereby answer two problems posed by Suciu\.
