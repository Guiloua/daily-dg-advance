# Determinantal representation of colored HOMFLY for double braids

- **作者：** Ya\. Kononov、A\. Morozov
- **arXiv：** [2609\.09437](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09437) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09437)
- **分类：** hep-th、math-ph、math\.GT
- **进展类型：** 新方法/构造
- **阅读优先级：** 58/100 · 中优先级
- **分析深度：** 摘要级分析

**完成的工作**

将矩形表示下双辫及扭结的彩色 HOMFLY-PT 多项式重写为行列式。

**使用技术**

- 微分展开
- KNTZ 演化公式重求和
- 单钩系数

**可能的突破**

给出较紧凑的矩阵表达，为与可积系统的联系提供起点。

**限制与不确定性**

KP/Toda 扩展尚属研究方向；非矩形表示及一般结族仍未解决。 当前为摘要级分析，技术细节与结论须回查原文。

**排序理由**

有利于结不变量计算，但适用结族与表示受限。

**原始英文摘要**

Starting from the known differential expansions, we express the HOMFLY-PT polynomials of twist and antiparallel double-braid knots in rectangular representations $[r^s]$ as determinants of $r\times r$ and $s\times s$ matrices\. We first give an elementary derivation for the figure-eight knot and then re-sum the KNTZ formulas for twist and double-braid evolution\. The resulting matrices are constructed from single-hook evolution coefficients and explicit representation-dependent weights\. These formulas provide a starting point for investigating possible extensions of knot polynomials to KP/Toda $τ$-functions\. Extensions to non-rectangular representations and to more general knot families remain open problems\.
