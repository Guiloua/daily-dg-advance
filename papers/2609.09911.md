# The geometric convergence of a parallel domain decomposition method on manifolds

- **作者：** Lizhen Qin
- **arXiv：** [2609\.09911](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09911) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09911)
- **分类：** math\.NA、math\.DG
- **进展类型：** 推广/加强
- **阅读优先级：** 64/100 · 中优先级
- **分析深度：** 摘要级分析

**完成的工作**

证明流形上连续并行区域分解椭圆求解法以几何速度收敛，并估计收敛率。

**使用技术**

- 椭圆方程
- 区域分解
- 收敛率估计

**可能的突破**

将已知收敛提升为定量几何收敛。

**限制与不确定性**

连续算法的结果不能直接当作任意网格离散实现的复杂度保证。 当前为摘要级分析，技术细节与结论须回查原文。

**排序理由**

可为几何 PDE 数值方法提供依据，理论突破集中在收敛率。

**原始英文摘要**

This paper establishes a convergence theory for a continuous domain decomposition method for elliptic equations on manifolds\. This method originated in \\cite\{lions2\} in the setting of Euclidean domains and was later adapted and generalized to manifolds by \\cite\{qin\_wang\_wang\}\. Although its convergence was known, whether the convergence is geometric has remained open\. We prove its geometric convergence and provide various estimates on the convergence rate\.
