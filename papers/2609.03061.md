# Maslov Indicies In Symplectic Geometry Revisited

- **作者：** Sylvain E\. Cappell、Edward Y\. Miller
- **arXiv：** [2609\.03061](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03061) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.03061)
- **分类：** math\.DG
- **进展类型：** 新方法/构造
- **阅读优先级：** 56/100 · 中优先级
- **分析深度：** 摘要级分析

**完成的工作**

在既有 Maslov 指标公理化框架中补充两种定义，并与由辛矩阵路径得到的近期指标构造进行比较。

**使用技术**

- 拉格朗日子空间路径
- Maslov 指标公理化
- 辛矩阵谱方法
- 指标比较

**可能的突破**

扩展了同一公理体系下的可选实现，并澄清经典 Maslov 指标与近期矩阵路径不变量之间的关系。

**限制与不确定性**

工作偏基础整理和等价性比较，摘要未显示对新的几何应用或尖锐估计有直接推进。

**排序理由**

是可靠的辛几何基础工具，但相对于曲率、几何流和度量几何主线，影响范围较专门。

**原始英文摘要**

Thirty years ago in \`\`On the Maslov Index'' the present authors with their late collaborator Prof\. Ronnie Lee of Yale University presented an axiomatization of the Maslov index, an integer-valued invariant of continuous, piecewise smooth paths of pairs of Lagrangians\. Four distinct methods of definition of such Maslov indicies were given; each satisfying the axioms\. Here two more are offered and comparisons are made to the recent work of Her and Zhong defining an invariant of a path of symplectic matrices motivated by earlier work of Lidskii and Gelfand, Salomon and Zehnder\.
