# Overlap-Helly theorems

- **作者：** Andreas F\. Holmsen、Alfredo Hubard
- **arXiv：** [2609\.10023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10023) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.10023)
- **分类：** math\.CO、cs\.CG、math\.GT
- **进展类型：** 新方法/构造
- **阅读优先级：** 71/100 · 中优先级
- **分析深度：** 摘要级分析

**完成的工作**

建立 overlap-Helly 框架，使拓扑彩色 Helly 与拓扑中心点结论成为共同推论。

**使用技术**

- 拓扑重叠定理
- 单纯复形
- 分数 Helly 方法

**可能的突破**

统一若干选择引理并处理稠密复形的连续第二选择问题。

**限制与不确定性**

稠密复形结论要求 tame 映射，不能不加限制推广。 当前为摘要级分析，技术细节与结论须回查原文。

**排序理由**

统一框架具组合几何工具价值，与曲率分析联系间接。

**原始英文摘要**

In this paper we introduce a generalization of Helly's theorem closely connected to Bárány-Gromov overlap theorems \(also called selection lemmas\)\. Our main result implies both the topological colorful Helly of Kalai and Meschulam and Karasev's topological centerpoint theorem\. We further investigate the topological fractional Helly theorem from this overlap perspective, and show an overlap theorem for dense complexes \(a continuous second selection lemma for tame maps\)\.
