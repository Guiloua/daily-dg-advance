# New Homogeneous Einstein Metrics from Detection

**完成的工作**

用“检测器”消元方法在多族紧齐性空间上构造并区分非对称 Einstein 度量，得到若干尖锐存在阈值及四因子空间上的 3\+1 与 2\+2 两种对称破缺类型。

**使用技术**

- 不变 Einstein 方程
- 对称破缺参数
- 多项式消元与检测器
- 齐性度量等距分类

**可能的突破**

不仅给出新度量，还在 SU\(18\)^4/ΔSp\(9\) 的固定族中精确得到三个两两非等距、不可约且非自然约化的非对称 Einstein 度量。

**限制与不确定性**

构造局限于列出的齐性空间与固定对称族；检测器的全局完备性依赖逐族代数证书。

**排序理由**

显式扩展齐性 Einstein 度量图谱，检测器兼具构造和唯一性判别能力。

**原始英文摘要**

We use a detection method for invariant Einstein equations to construct and distinguish asymmetric homogeneous Einstein metrics on several families of compact homogeneous spaces\. The method retains a geometrically meaningful symmetry-breaking parameter, eliminates the remaining Einstein equations, and then reconstructs positive metrics from the resulting detector\. We prove a sharp threshold in a two-factor symplectic family, construct two asymmetric Einstein metrics on each admissible space $\\SU\(n\)^3/Δ\\SO\(n\)$, obtain a sharp threshold for $\\SU\(2n\)^3/Δ\\Sp\(n\)$, and construct asymmetric metrics on three exceptional three-factor families\. The four-factor case exhibits a further phenomenon\. On \\\[ M\_9=\\SU\(18\)^4/Δ\\Sp\(9\) \\\] the two symmetry types $3\+1$ and $2\+2$ are both realized: up to homothety and internal block permutations, the corresponding fixed families contain exactly three asymmetric Einstein metrics, two with three equal horizontal scales and one distinct scale \(type $3\+1$\), and one with two equal pairs of horizontal scales \(type $2\+2$\)\. They are pairwise non-isometric, Riemannian irreducible, and not naturally reductive\. The $2\+2$ metric is globally unique in the full positive $2\+2$ fixed family up to block interchange\.
