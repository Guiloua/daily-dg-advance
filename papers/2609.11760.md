**原始英文摘要**

Let $B\subset\mathbb{C}^n$ be a ball centred at the origin and $D=\{z^1=0\}$\. Let $g$ be a Kähler metric of constant holomorphic sectional curvature \(chsc\) on $B\setminus D$, uniformly equivalent to the model cone metric of angle $2πβ$, with $0&lt;β&lt;1$, and polyhomogeneous along $D$\. We prove that, in suitable holomorphic coordinates $(w^1, \ldots, w^n)$ centred at the origin, the metric $g$ is the pullback of the corresponding complex space form by the map $(w^1,w^2,\dots,w^n)\longmapsto \bigl((w^1)^β,w^2,\dots,w^n\bigr)$\.
