**原始英文摘要**

We study Kähler metrics with cone angle $2πβ$ along a smooth divisor, in the range $1/2&lt;β&lt;1$\. We show that bounded Riemann curvature near the divisor forces the normal bundle sequence to split holomorphically, confirming a condition proposed by Donaldson\.
