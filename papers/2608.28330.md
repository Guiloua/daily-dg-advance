# Realising atomless laws as distance distributions on metric measure spaces

- **作者：** Christoph Thäle、Philipp Tuchel
- **arXiv：** [2608\.28330](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28330) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.28330)
- **分类：** math\.PR、math\.MG
- **进展类型：** 距离分布实现问题的完整刻画
- **阅读优先级：** 90/100 · 高优先级
- **分析深度：** 已补读正文关键部分

**完成的工作**

证明 \[0,∞\) 上无原子的 Borel 概率测度可实现为完备可分度量概率空间内两独立采样点的距离分布，当且仅当其支撑含 0；实现度量还可对任意 L&gt;1 取为与某超度量 L-bi-Lipschitz 等价。

**使用技术**

- 无原子测度分拆
- 紧致分量的度量粘合
- 尺度递归分配
- 近超度量构造

**可能的突破**

解决 Aldous–Blanc–Curien 对绝对连续距离分布的猜想，并覆盖奇异无原子测度；克服直观超度量模型不可分的障碍。

**限制与不确定性**

结论仅刻画两点距离的单一边缘分布，不决定整个度量测度空间；含原子一般分布和三点联合距离分布不能据此一并解决。近超度量不等于可分超度量本身。

**排序理由**

基础逆问题获得简洁充要条件，构造方法对度量概率模型有广泛启发；限制清晰且结果超出原猜想范围，列为度量测度方向首读。

**原始英文摘要**

We study which probability measures on $\[0,\\infty\)$ can occur as the distribution of the distance between two independent points sampled from a complete separable metric space equipped with a Borel probability measure\. We prove that an atomless Borel probability measure can be realised in this way if and only if its support contains the origin\. This settles the conjecture of Aldous, Blanc, and Curien for absolutely continuous laws and also covers atomless measures that are singular with respect to Lebesgue measure\. Moreover, for every $L&gt;1$, we show that the realising metric may be chosen $L$-bi-Lipschitz equivalent to an ultrametric\. The proof constructs the space from compact components whose mutual distances produce prescribed parts of the measure, while distances within the components are assigned to smaller scales\.
