# Rigidity and flexibility under positive isotropic curvature

- **作者：** Tsz-Kiu Aaron Chow、Yipeng Wang
- **arXiv：** [2609\.11702](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11702) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.11702)

**完成的工作**

在球面上构造固定 PIC 下界但 Urysohn 宽度及稳定极小盘内半径任意大的度量，并给出偶维二形式谱锐界。

**使用技术**

- 几何构造
- 二形式谱估计

**可能的突破**

声称否定宽度与稳定盘半径猜想，同时给出附加秩条件下的等号刚性。

**限制与不确定性**

PIC 不等于截面曲率正；谱等号刚性要求闭特征形式的秩条件。 已补读引言或主结果片段，尚未逐行验证完整证明。

**排序理由**

曲率条件究竟控制哪些大尺度量，直接关系比较几何主线。

**原始英文摘要**

For every $n\ge4$ and $L&gt;0$, we construct a smooth $4$-PIC metric on $S^n$ with Urysohn $1$-width at least $L$ and an embedded stable minimal disk of intrinsic inradius at least $L$\. These examples disprove the proposed width and stable-disk radius bounds under a positive lower bound for isotropic curvature\. On closed even-dimensional manifolds, we prove the sharp estimate $λ_1^{(2)}\ge(n-1)σ/2$ under $σ$-PIC and show that equality forces roundness if a closed eigenform attaining the bound has rank at least four at some point\.
