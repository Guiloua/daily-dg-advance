# Conservation of mass for solutions of Leibenson's equation on Riemannian Manifolds

- **作者：** Philipp Sürig
- **arXiv：** [2609\.02732](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02732) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02732)
- **分类：** math\.AP、math\.DG
- **进展类型：** 守恒判据/反例
- **阅读优先级：** 68/100 · 中优先级
- **分析深度：** 摘要级分析

**完成的工作**

为流形上的 Leibenson 方程建立质量守恒的体积增长条件，分别处理退化与快扩散范围，并在双曲空间给出快扩散有限消失。

**使用技术**

- 非线性扩散与 p-Laplacian
- 体积增长判据
- 质量守恒与 Liouville 性质

**可能的突破**

快扩散的多项式增长阈值吻合欧氏情形，并从临界守恒结果得到部分 Liouville 猜想进展。

**限制与不确定性**

依赖参数 q\(p−1\) 所在范围和增长条件；双曲反例表明不能忽略远处体积增长。

**排序理由**

把大尺度几何直接连接到非线性守恒律，判据可供相关 PDE 课题复用，但问题模型较具体。

**原始英文摘要**

We consider on a Riemannian manifold $M$ the Leibenson equation \\begin\{equation\*\}\\label\{eqabs\}\\partial \_\{t\}u=Δ\_\{p\}u^\{q\},\\end\{equation\*\} where $p&gt;1$ and $q&gt;0$\. When $q(p-1)\geq 1$, we prove conservation of mass for solutions of Leibenson's equation assuming only the volume bound $V(x_0, r)\leq \exp\left(C r^{\frac{p}{p-1}}\right)$ for some $x_{0}\in M$ and all large enough $r&gt;0$\. When $q\(p-1\)&lt; 1$, we prove this property assuming $V(x_0, r)\leq Cr^{N}$ and $p&gt;N\[1-q\(p-1\)\]$, which matches the threshold in $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ with $N=n$\. We also show that solutions on the hyperbolic space $\mathbb{H}^{n}$ have a finite extinction time in the case $q\(p-1\)&lt; 1$, which implies that the conservation of mass property does not hold\. Using the conservation of mass result in the case $q(p-1)=1$, we also prove a $L^{p-1}$- Liouville property, which partially answers a conjecture stated by I\. Holopainen \\cite\{holopainen2000sharp\}\.
