**原始英文摘要**

We study the infinite-dimensional Gaussian pyramid and its quotients by the global sign flip and the $U(1)$-Hopf action\. We resolve affirmatively a long-standing problem posed by Tomohiro Fukaya around 2014: these three limiting geometries are pairwise non-similar, meaning that no positive rescaling makes any two of them coincide\.
