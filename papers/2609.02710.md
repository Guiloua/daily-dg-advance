# Asymptotic mean value Laplacian on equiregular sub-Riemannian manifolds

- **作者：** Fabrice Baudoin、Jonathan Junné、David Tewodrose
- **arXiv：** [2609\.02710](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02710) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02710)
- **分类：** math\.DG
- **进展类型：** 算子极限/判据
- **阅读优先级：** 73/100 · 中优先级
- **分析深度：** 摘要级分析

**完成的工作**

刻画等正则亚黎曼流形度量球均值算子的点态小尺度极限：一般情形等价于重标度水平一阶矩收敛，步数至多二时验证该条件。

**使用技术**

- 切向 Carnot 球矩
- 实解析有限 jet 化简
- tame 积分与分布收敛

**可能的突破**

把极限存在与几何球矩明确对应，并给出主符号、换测度漂移公式以及任意等正则情形的加权分布收敛。

**限制与不确定性**

高步数流形的无条件点态收敛仍未由摘要保证；分布收敛与点态结论不能混同。

**排序理由**

亚黎曼分析的可复用结构判据，同时明确高步数障碍；技术价值高，读者应先区分两类收敛。

**原始英文摘要**

Let $(M,\mathcal{D},g)$ be a smooth equiregular sub-Riemannian manifold equipped with a smooth positive measure $μ$\. We study the small-scale limit of the metric-ball mean-value operator 

$$
A_hf(x)=\frac{1}{h^{2}μ(B(x,h)) }\int_{B(x,h)}(f(q)-f(x))\, dμ(q).
$$

 Exact homogeneity yields the pointwise limit on Carnot groups\. On a general equiregular manifold, convergence for every smooth test function is equivalent to convergence of the rescaled horizontal first moments of metric balls in first-kind privileged coordinates\. This criterion is independent of $μ$; when it holds, the principal symbol is determined by the normalized second-moment tensor of the tangent unit ball, and the drift satisfies an explicit change-of-measure formula\. In step at most two, we verify the criterion by combining a real-analytic finite-jet reduction with tame integration, which rules out oscillation of the normalized moments\. We also compute the limit on Lie groups and prove unconditional distributional convergence of the volume-weighted operators on every equiregular manifold\.
