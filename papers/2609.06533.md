**原始英文摘要**

A complete one-ended three-manifold with strictly positive Ricci curvature is constructed such that 

$$
\limsup_{R\to\infty}\frac1R\int_{B(p,R)} Scal\,dV=+\infty.
$$

 The construction combines an explicit toric lens carrying a large scalar-curvature integral with a three-dimensional angular pair of pants and an adaptive sequence of hybrid blocks\.
