**原始英文摘要**

Let $(M^m,g)$, $m\geq2$, be a closed connected Riemannian manifold with $\secg_g\leq-1$, and let $(X,g_X)$ be its universal cover\. Yau proved that $\hiso(X)\geq m-1$\. We prove that equality is rigid: if $\hiso(X)=m-1$, then $(X,g_X)$ is isometric to $\Hh^m(-1)$\.
