**原始英文摘要**

For every $1\\leq p&lt;\\infty$ and even integer $m\geq4$, we determine the optimal order of the $p$-moment torus inequality for $L_1$: it is $m^p n^{(1-p/2)_+}+n$, with comparison constants independent of $p,m,n$ after taking $p$-th roots\. Consequently, for every $2\\leq q&lt;\\infty$ and $1\leq p\leq q$, the corresponding metric cotype inequality holds at the sharp scale $m=O(n^{1/q})$\. In particular, the quadratic inequality holds with $m=O(\sqrt{n})$, answering the $L_1$ sharp metric cotype question of Mendel and Naor\. The proof combines finite cut representations with a nonlinear smoothing estimate and an exact ternary rounding identity\.
