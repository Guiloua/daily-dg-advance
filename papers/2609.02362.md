# Farthest-cell triplet entropy: high-dimensional shell limits and hyperbolic curvature amplification

- **作者：** Chongkun Deng
- **arXiv：** [2609\.02362](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02362) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02362)
- **分类：** math\.PR、math\.MG

- **阅读优先级：** 47/100 · 低阅读优先级

**完成的工作**

引入只记录最远原型标签的三元熵，推导高维径向模型的欧氏与双曲极限，并讨论经模型校准的曲率反演。

**使用技术**

- 条件 Shannon 熵
- 高维径向极限
- 模型校准与模拟

**可能的突破**

在特定随机模型中用比较标签压缩几何信息，并明确双曲曲率对有效径向尺度的放大关系。

**限制与不确定性**

曲率恢复不是纯图推断，需径向分布和尺度校准；摘要中的合成实验显示对角向各向异性明显不稳健。

**排序理由**

适合几何统计的探索性阅读；对一般度量曲率理论的直接适用性有限。

主要结果依赖各向同性径向模型及外部尺度，尚不能直接用于一般曲率判定；因此在本期基础几何结果之后，不代表论文质量判断。

**原始英文摘要**

We introduce farthest-cell triplet entropy, the conditional Shannon entropy of the farthest-prototype label given three random prototypes\. For independent queries and prototypes, its estimator records only the farthest label, not coordinates or numerical distances\. The statistic is bounded by $\\log 3$, is invariant under common strictly increasing transformations of the dissimilarities, and has an exact mutual-information interpretation\. In high-dimensional isotropic radial models $X\_d=R\_dU\_d$, the Euclidean ordering reduces to scores $λ\_dξ\_\{i,d\}-Z\_i$, where $λ\_d=\\sqrt d\\,\\operatorname\{sd\}\(R\_d\)/\\mathbb\{E\} R\_d$ and the $Z\_i$ are independent standard Gaussian variables\. This gives angular-dominated, intermediate, and radial-dominated entropy limits $\\log 3$, $H\_\{\\infty\}\(λ;F\)$, and $0$\. In hyperbolic space of curvature $-κ\_d^2$, the same master curve appears at $λ\_\{d,\\mathbb H\}=\\sqrt d\\,τ\_d A\(s\_d\)$, where $τ\_d=\\operatorname\{sd\}\(R\_d\)/\\mathbb\{E\} R\_d$, $s\_d=κ\_d\\mathbb\{E\} R\_d$, and $A\(s\)=s\\coth s$\. With a calibrated radial law and $τ\_d$, and a monotone operating interval, entropy inversion identifies the scale-invariant target $s\_d^2$; absolute curvature requires an external length unit\. CPU simulations give Euclidean and hyperbolic master-curve RMSEs of $0\.0164$ and $0\.0209$\. Inversion from observed synthetic latent coordinates has a median relative error in $κ$ of $6\.6\\%$, while angular anisotropy increases this error to $68\.9\\%$\. Thus the entropy statistic is comparison-based, whereas curvature recovery remains model-calibrated, is not graph-only, and is not robust to anisotropy\.
