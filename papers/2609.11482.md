# Commensurability Relations Between Deligne-Mostow-Thurston and Ghazouani-Pirio Monodromy Groups

- **作者：** Chenglong Yu、Yiming Zhong
- **arXiv：** [2609\.11482](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11482) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.11482)
- **分类：** math\.GT、math\.AG
- **进展类型：** 新定理/分类
- **阅读优先级：** 62/100 · 中优先级

**完成的工作**

分类十六个算术 Ghazouani–Pirio 群与 Deligne–Mostow–Thurston 群的公度关系。

**使用技术**

- 退化计算

**限制与不确定性**

**排序理由**

**原始英文摘要**

We classify commensurability relations between Deligne--Mostow--Thurston monodromy groups and the sixteen arithmetic Ghazouani--Pirio monodromy groups arising from moduli of flat cone metrics on the sphere and on the torus\. In both settings, the monodromy group preserves a skew-Hermitian form coming from twisted homology\. We use a degeneration method to compute their determinant classes in the genus one case\. The defining CM fields and determinant classes of the skew-Hermitian forms determine the commensurability relations\. In particular, each of the sixteen arithmetic Ghazouani--Pirio monodromy groups is commensurable with an arithmetic Deligne--Mostow--Thurston monodromy group\.
