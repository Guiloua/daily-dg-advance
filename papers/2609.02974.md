# Quilt decompositions of surfaces and Torelli group action on the extended Hatcher complex

- **作者：** Hiromasa Fuchizawa
- **arXiv：** [2609\.02974](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02974) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02974)
- **分类：** math\.GT
- **进展类型：** 新方法/构造
- **阅读优先级：** 66/100 · 中优先级
- **分析深度：** 摘要级分析

**完成的工作**

为带“缝线”的曲面裤分解及其着色版本建立变换群胚的表示，并构造连通、单连通的二维胞腔复形；进一步证明 Torelli 群的一个无挠子群在这些复形上自由作用。

**使用技术**

- 裤分解与缝线结构
- 二维胞腔复形
- 群胚表示
- 映射类群作用

**可能的突破**

把 Hatcher 型复形扩展到更细的 quilt 数据，并把 Torelli 群作用转化为可计算的自由胞腔作用，为研究其群论性质提供新模型。

**限制与不确定性**

结论聚焦特定分解复形和无挠子群；对整个 Torelli 群的几何或同调后果仍需进一步提取。

**排序理由**

对低维拓扑与映射类群具有直接价值，构造可复用，但与曲率和度量极限的主线关联较弱。

**原始英文摘要**

We give a presentation of a groupoid of transformations of quilt decompositions of a surface, which are pants decompositions of the surface with the addition of "seams"\. We consider colored quilt decompositions of a surface, where one side of a quilt decomposition is colored\. We also give a presentation of a groupoid of transformations of colored quilt decompositions of a surface\. These presentations of such groupoids are realized by constructing connected and simply-connected two-dimensional cell complexes whose 0-cell are these up-to-isotopy structures on the surface\. In the latter part of this paper, we show that Torelli group \(or more precisely, a torsion-free subgroup of a mapping class group of a surface\) acts on these complexes freely\.
