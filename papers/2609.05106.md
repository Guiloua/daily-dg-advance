**完成的工作**

从 Calabi 曲率算子的不同部分正性推出标量、Ricci 与正交 Ricci 曲率正性，并给出紧 Kähler–Einstein 流形的谱夹逼刚性。

**使用技术**

- 曲率算子特征值
- 部分正性
- Kähler–Einstein 条件

**可能的突破**

把算子谱不等式联系到常全纯截面曲率，为 Kähler 刚性提供定量判据。

**限制与不确定性**

需核对 θ\(n,α\) 的具体定义和归一化；紧致 Einstein 假设不可删去。 本条基于已检查来源的阅读分析，关键结论请回查原文。

**原始英文摘要**

In this paper, we study the Calabi curvature operator on K\{ä\}hler manifolds\. First, we prove that if the Calabi curvature operator on K\{ä\}hler manifolds satisfies $\frac{n\left(n + 1\right)}{2}$-positive \(nonnegative\), $\frac{n + 1}{2}$-positive \(nonnegative\), and $\left( n-1 \right)$-positive \(nonnegative\), then the scalar curvature, Ricci curvature, and orthogonal Ricci curvature are positive \(nonnegative\), respectively\. Second, we show that any compact K\{ä\}hler Einstein manifold satisfying the condition 

$$
λ_1+\dots+λ_α\ge -αθ(n,α)\barλ,\; α\le \frac{n}{2}
$$

 must have nonnegative constant holomorphic sectional curvature\.
