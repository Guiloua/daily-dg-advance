# Discrepancy of geometric incidences

- **作者：** Azem Adibelli、István Tomon
- **arXiv：** [2608\.28354](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28354) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.28354)
- **分类：** math\.CO、cs\.CC、cs\.DM、math\.MG
- **进展类型：** 几何 discrepancy 的多项式改进
- **阅读优先级：** 77/100 · 高优先级

**完成的工作**

为实 Euclidean 空间有限点集相对有界维数、次数仿射代数集的红蓝 discrepancy 给出超过直接 VC 维数界的多项式改进，同时构造高维超平面 discrepancy 下界并给出通信复杂度应用。

**使用技术**

- 多项式分割与分割树
- 几何关联图
- 代数集 spanning 结构
- discrepancy 与通信复杂度

**可能的突破**

利用实代数几何中 VC 维数未表达的结构，取得额外正指数 ε 的改进，并将平面点线下界拓展到高维超平面。

**限制与不确定性**

高维上下界尚未匹配，文中明确把缩小差距留作问题；ε 与维数及次数有关。结论针对代数集关联，不应泛化到所有半代数范围或半空间。

**排序理由**

定量改进实质且技术跨组合、计算和几何，值得工具导向读者关注；对核心微分几何课题较间接，因此排在结构性几何突破之后。

**原始英文摘要**

We study the combinatorial \(red-blue\) discrepancy of finite point sets with respect to hyperplanes and, more generally, bounded-complexity affine algebraic sets\. We prove that every $n$-point set in a real Euclidean space admits a red-blue coloring for which every affine algebraic set of dimension at most $D$ and degree at most $k$ has discrepancy at most $n^\{\\frac12-\\frac\{1\}\{2\(D\+1\)\}-\\varepsilon\}$ for some $\\varepsilon=\\varepsilon\(D,k\)&gt;0$\. This gives a polynomial improvement over the straightforward VC-dimension bound $\\tilde O\(n^\{\\frac12-\\frac\{1\}\{2\(D\+1\)\}\}\)$\. In the opposite direction, we construct $n$-point sets in $\\mathbb R^d$ whose discrepancy with respect to hyperplanes is $\\tildeΩ\(n^\{\\frac12-\\frac\{1\}\{d\+1\}\}\),$ extending the point-line discrepancy lower bound of Chazelle and Lvov\. We present further applications of our methods in communication complexity, concerning separation between randomized communication cost and deterministic communication cost with access to equality oracle\.
