# The real quotient of the Cartwright-Steger surface

- **arXiv：** [2609\.09207](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09207) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09207)

**完成的工作**

确定 Cartwright–Steger 曲面复共轭商的四维拓扑类型，并描述其分支覆盖。

**使用技术**

- 反全纯 involution
- 四维流形拓扑
- 分支覆盖

**可能的突破**

将商空间识别为 CP² 与 S¹×S³ 的连通和，分支集为三个实射影平面的连通和。

**限制与不确定性**

商空间的同胚分类不应表述为已确定所有光滑结构。 当前为摘要级分析，技术细节与结论须回查原文。

**排序理由**

为特殊复曲面提供具体拓扑模型，影响主要集中在四维几何拓扑。

**原始英文摘要**

Borisov and Yeung showed that the Cartwright-Steger surface $X$ is defined over ${\mathbb {Q}}$, hence complex conjugation gives an antiholomorphic involution on $X$\. We show that the orbit space $Y$ is a closed oriented smooth four-manifold homeomorphic to ${\mathbb {CP}}^2\# (S^1 \times S^3)$, presenting $X$ as a double branched cover $X\to Y$, with branch locus diffeomorphic to the connected sum of three copies of the real projective plane\.
