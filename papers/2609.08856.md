# Global rigidity of sphere packings in constant curvature background geometry

**完成的工作**

用共形四面体的对偶刻画证明常曲率背景球堆积刚性，并将变分原理推广到三流形。

**使用技术**

- 延拓变分原理

**限制与不确定性**

**排序理由**

离散曲率及几何变分直接相关。

**原始英文摘要**

In this paper, we study the dual characterization of conformal tetrahedra in constant curvature spaces, which develops a geometric technique to obtain the global rigidity of hyperbolic, Euclidean, spherical and ideal hyperbolic sphere packings on a tetrahedron\. Moreover, we establish the extended variational principle to obtain the global rigidity of the \(ideal\) hyperbolic sphere packings on 3-manifolds\. We also generalize the conformal tetrahedra to higher dimensions and provide a characterization of conformal $n(\ge3)$-simplices in constant curvature spaces, which is useful for studying higher-dimensional sphere packings\.
