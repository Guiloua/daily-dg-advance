# Conformal boundary rigidity for simple Finsler metrics

- **arXiv：** [2609\.09527](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09527) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09527)
- **分类：** math\.DG、math-ph
- **进展类型：** 推广/加强
- **阅读优先级：** 81/100 · 高优先级

**完成的工作**

证明简单 Finsler 流形在共形类中的边界距离稳定性，并处理距离数据的奇性。

**使用技术**

- Mukhometov 分部积分
- Finsler 测地线分析
- 适配 H² 范数

**可能的突破**

把经典边界刚性稳定估计推广到 Finsler 设定。

**限制与不确定性**

简单性与指定共形因子类不可省略，未解决一般 Finsler 边界刚性。 已补读相关引言或主结果片段，尚未逐行验证完整证明。

**排序理由**

刚性与逆问题技术相关性高，Sobolev 数据范数处理可复用。

**原始英文摘要**

In this paper we prove that simple Finsler manifolds are conformally stable\. Given a simple Finsler manifold and a class of conformal factors, we characterize the singularity of their induced boundary distance functions which allows us to define an appropiate $H^2$ norm\. We then obtain a stability estimate with respect to this Sobolev norm and the $L^2$ norm on the class of the conformal factors\. To prove the main theorems, we adapt the classical integration by parts technique employed by Mukhometov \\cite\{Muhometov\} to the Finsler setting\.
