**原始英文摘要**

For every $n\geq2$, we consider a Gaussian zonoid of revolution $Z_n$ arising from works of Vitale and Mathis\. We prove that $Z_n^*$ is also a zonoid and compute the Banach--Mazur distance from $Z_n$ to the Euclidean ball\. This distance is independent of the dimension and is approximately $1.10$\. Consequently, the supremal Banach--Mazur distance among zonoids whose polars are zonoids does not converge to $1$\. The construction also produces a separable real Banach space $X$, not isometric to a Hilbert space, such that both $X$ and $X^*$ embed linearly isometrically into $L_1$\.
