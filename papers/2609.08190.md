**原始英文摘要**

A finite set of unit vectors is $k$-irreducible if every signed sum of between two and $k$ distinct elements has norm greater than one\. Let $\mathcal{R}_k$ be the maximal asymptotic rate of such sets, and let $κ(α)$ be the maximal asymptotic rate of spherical codes with pairwise inner products at most $α$\. For $k \ge 2$ we show: \\begin\{align\} \\mathcal\{R\}\_k \\le \\min\_\{1 \\le r \\le \\lfloor k/2 \\rfloor\} \\frac\{1\}\{r\} \\, κ\\\!\\left\(1 - \\frac\{1\}\{2r\}\\right\) \\, \. \\end\{align\} Combining this with standard sphere packing bounds, for large $k$ we obtain an almost-tight asymptotic comparison with the known lower bounds: \\begin\{align\} \\left\(\\tfrac\{1\}\{2\}-o\(1\)\\right\) \\, \\frac\{\\log\_2 k\}\{k\} \\le \\mathcal\{R\}\_k \\le \(1 \+ o\(1\)\) \\, \\frac\{\\log\_2 k\}\{k\} \\, \. \\end\{align\}
