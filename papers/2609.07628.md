**原始英文摘要**

We develop a framework for studying discrete subgroups of $\mathsf{PGL}(d+1,\mathbb{R})$ via the paracomplex hyperbolic space $\mathbb{H}_τ^d$, a rank-$1$ pseudo-Riemannian symmetric space\. We characterize projective transverse, relatively Anosov, and Anosov subgroups in terms of properly discontinuous, geometrically finite, and convex-cocompact actions respectively on their weak hulls, which are canonical flow spaces in the spacelike unit tangent bundle of $\mathbb{H}_τ^d$\. A key ingredient is the construction of a Busemann-type horofunction on the spacelike unit tangent bundle with the properties needed to describe cuspidal geometry\. We further prove for relatively Anosov subgroups that the geodesic flows on their weak hulls are uniformly hyperbolic, giving a relative analogue of the Axiom A property\.
