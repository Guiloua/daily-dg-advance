# A Bernstein problem for translating solutions to the mean curvature flow

- **作者：** Juan Davila、Manuel del Pino、Juncheng Wei
- **arXiv：** [2609\.04675](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04675) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.04675)

**完成的工作**

构造每个 N≥8 中一参数族整图平均曲率流平移解，具有平均凸性但非凸性。

**使用技术**

- BDG 极小图
- 奇异摄动
- Simons 锥过渡层
- 加权 Jacobi 理论
- 全局障碍

**可能的突破**

在 Bernstein 临界维数展示平均凸平移解的新非刚性，并以抛物型内层匹配处理破坏奇对称的平移项。

**限制与不确定性**

构造针对高维，未分类所有整图平移解，也未解决中间维数的凸性问题。 本条基于已检查来源的阅读分析，关键结论请回查原文。 正文注明该结果曾于 2011 年宣布，应区分本次完整证明发表与结果最初宣布时间。

**排序理由**

几何流模型与高维极小曲面直接相连，技术路线和非刚性结果均值得优先研究。

**原始英文摘要**

We study entire graphical translating solutions of the mean curvature flow, \\\[ \\operatorname\{div\}\\\!\\left\(\\frac\{\\nabla G\}\{\\sqrt\{1\+\|\\nabla G\|^2\}\}\\right\) =\\frac\{1\}\{\\sqrt\{1\+\|\\nabla G\|^2\}\} \\qquad\\text\{in \}\\mathbb R^N\. \\\] Every such graph is mean-convex, since its mean curvature is the vertical component of its unit normal\. In dimension two, mean-convex translating solitons are convex, and an entire graphical translator is therefore the rotationally symmetric bowl soliton\. In higher dimensions Wang constructed non-rotational entire convex translating graphs\. We prove that a further loss of rigidity occurs at the Bernstein dimension: for every $N\\ge 8$ there exists a one-parameter family of entire graphical translators that are mean-convex but not convex\. The construction starts from the Bombieri--De Giorgi--Giusti \(BDG\) entire minimal graph in $\\mathbb R^8$ and develops a singular perturbation theory for the translator equation around it\. The main new feature is a transition layer near Simons' cone: the translating term breaks the odd symmetry of the minimal graph, and after a suitable recentering the matching problem is governed by a parabolic inner equation\. A detailed analysis of this layer, together with weighted Jacobi theory on the BDG graph and global barriers, yields the desired entire solutions\.
