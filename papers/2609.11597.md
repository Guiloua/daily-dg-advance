# Non-trivial ABC-Massey products on complex parallelisable solvmanifolds

- **作者：** Nunzia Cesarino、Tommaso Sferruzza、Adriano Tomassini
- **arXiv：** [2609\.11597](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11597) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.11597)
- **分类：** math\.DG
- **进展类型：** 新定理/分类
- **阅读优先级：** 66/100 · 中优先级
- **分析深度：** 摘要级分析

**完成的工作**

在非交换复幺模可解 Lie 代数上构造非零三重 ABC-Massey 积，并应用到复平行化 solvmanifold。

**使用技术**

- Bott–Chern 与 Aeppli 上同调
- Massey 操作

**可能的突破**

提供 pluripotential 同伦非平凡性的系统性例子。

**限制与不确定性**

非交换前提不可省略，交换的复环面须单独区分。 当前为摘要级分析，方法及结论须回查原文。

**排序理由**

复几何高阶不变量明确，适合专门方向阅读。

**原始英文摘要**

Triple Aeppli--Bott--Chern--Massey products, shortly, triple ABC-Massey products are higher-order operations on the Bott--Chern and Aeppli cohomologies of a compact complex manifold, and their non-vanishing is an invariant of its pluripotential homotopy type\. We prove that every non-Abelian complex unimodular solvable Lie algebra admits a non-vanishing triple ABC--Massey product, and we deduce that so does every compact complex parallelisable solvmanifold\.
