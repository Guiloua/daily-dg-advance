# A Smooth Counterexample to the Trautman Conjecture

- **arXiv：** [2609\.03198](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03198) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.03198)

**完成的工作**

构造光滑、强拟凸、不可局部嵌入的三维 CR 流形，同时保留典范丛存在处处非零闭截面的条件，从而否定 Trautman 猜想。

**使用技术**

**原始英文摘要**

The Trautman conjecture asserted that a smooth three-dimensional CR manifold admitting a nowhere-zero closed section of its canonical bundle must be locally embeddable in $\\mathbb\{C\}^2$\. We modify a standard construction of nonembeddable smooth strongly pseudoconvex CR 3-manifolds so that the condition of having a nowhere-zero closed section of the canonical bundle is preserved, thus providing a strongly pseudoconvex counterexample to the Trautman conjecture\.
