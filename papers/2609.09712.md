# Obata-type equations under the Bakry-Émery Ricci curvature conditions

- **作者：** Mijia Lai、Yiwei Liu
- **arXiv：** [2609\.09712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09712) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09712)

**完成的工作**

研究带 Robin 边界条件的 Obata 型方程，在 Bakry–Émery 曲率控制下建立紧带边流形刚性。

**使用技术**

- Obata 型方程
- 扭曲积结构
- 加权 Ricci 曲率

**可能的突破**

**原始英文摘要**

In this paper, based on the warped product structures determined by the Obata-type equations with Robin boundary condition, we establish some rigidity results for compact manifolds with smooth boundary under appropriate Bakry-Émery Ricci curvature conditions and some other assumptions\.
