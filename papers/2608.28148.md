# Flows and Homotopies of Banach Lie algebroids

- **作者：** Fernand Pelletier、Alexander Schmeding
- **arXiv：** [2608\.28148](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28148) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.28148)
- **分类：** math\.DG
- **进展类型：** 无限维局部流与同伦基础
- **阅读优先级：** 68/100 · 中优先级
- **分析深度：** 已补读正文关键部分

**完成的工作**

在 Banach Lie algebroid 上建立参数依赖的局部截面延拓、完全提升及纤维线性流、Bochner 积分变常公式，并证明可容许路径的同伦、参数方形上的 algebroid 态射与输运方程之间的等价性。

**使用技术**

- 线性联络与平行移动
- 局部 addition
- 完全提升及纤维线性演化
- Bochner 积分变常公式

**可能的突破**

绕过一般 Banach 流形上缺少光滑 bump 函数和紧支延拓的问题，为无限维局部积分理论建立所需的无穷小工具。

**限制与不确定性**

参数统一延拓和同伦结论有联络及光滑仿紧性等条件；本文明确不构造积分的局部 Banach Lie groupoid，该工作留待后续。

**排序理由**

填补无限维微分几何常用论证中的基础空缺，技术严谨性值得关注；目前定位是后续积分计划的工具层，应用范围专门。

**原始英文摘要**

We establish flow and homotopy tools for Banach Lie algebroids that do not rely on compactly supported extensions\. Under suitable hypotheses, we construct parameter-dependent local extensions of sections along algebroid paths, complete lifts and their fibrewise-linear evolutions, and a Bochner-integral variation-of-constants formula\. We then prove that an admissible-path homotopy is equivalently a Lie algebroid morphism from the parameter square and a solution of the associated transport equation\. This provides the extension, transport and infinitesimal variation theory required for a local integration program of Banach Lie algebroids to be developed in a sequel\.
