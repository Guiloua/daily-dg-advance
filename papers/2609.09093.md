**原始英文摘要**

Jones described how the Ising and Potts models from statistical mechanics give rise, with appropriate choices of Boltzmann weights, to invariants of an oriented link\. The Boltzmann weights that Jones proposed, however, work only with a correction factor that he does not mention\. We fill in the missing details in two different ways\. We also show that all of these invariants, which we call the Ising and Potts invariants, are given by evaluating the Jones polynomial at $t$ equal to one of these Boltzmann weights\.
