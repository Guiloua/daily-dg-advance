**原始英文摘要**

We prove that the gradient graph of any $W^{2,1}$ semi-convex viscosity solution to the three-dimensional subcritical special Lagrangian equation is area-minimizing and smooth\. Furthermore, we prove the smoothness of $W^{2,1}\cap C^{1,1/3+}$ semi-convex viscosity solutions\. This yields progress on the conjecture proposed by Wang--Yuan \[WY13\]\. The corresponding results in higher dimensions are also established\.
