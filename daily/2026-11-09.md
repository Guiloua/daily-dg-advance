## On the Volume Conjectures for Dehn-filled $3$-manifolds along fundamental shadow link complements with big volume

We prove the Volume Conjecture and the Asymptotic Expansion Conjecture for closed oriented hyperbolic $3$-manifolds obtained by integral Dehn-fillings along a fundamental shadow link complement, provided that their volumes are sufficiently close to that of the complement. ## Bourgain's Theorem for a Union of Euclidean Metric Spaces

Dimitar Chakarov、Sepideh Mahabadi、Yury Makarychev、Ali Vakilian

设度量空间X=∪_(i=1)^k U_i，各U_i以诱导度量等距嵌入ℓ₂。定理1.2给c₂(X)=O(k^1.128 log k)，k=2时改进至c₂(X)<3.997。若每个U_i加入X中任意一点仍等距欧氏，则定理1.3给O(k^.895 log k)；加入任意两点仍欧氏时，定理1.1给渐近尖锐的O(log k)。

We study the distortion of embedding a union of $k$ Euclidean sets into Euclidean space. We show that a union of $k$ Euclidean sets can be embedded with distortion at most $O(k^{1.128} \log k)$, independent of the number of points. This is an exponential improvement over the previous best known upper bound of $2^{O(k)}$ obtained by iterating the result of Makarychev and Makarychev (2016) for the special case $k = 2$. Surprisingly, with the additional assumption that each Euclidean set remains Euclidean when we adjoin any other two points, we can obtain an asymptotically tight distortion of $O(\log k)$, thus, providing a generalization of Bourgain's Theorem (1985). Along the way, we also show that the distortion of the union can be upper bounded by $O(\log k)$ times the maximum of the Lipschitz extension constants of individual Euclidean sets.

Caiyan Li

## Fractional Fefferman--Stein inequalities on infinite rooted $k$-ary trees

Gonzalo Ibañez-Firnkorn、Jorgelina Recchi

对无限根k叉树的中心分数阶极大算子，作者证明s>1的Fefferman–Stein弱型估计，常数与分叉参数k无关。

We study weighted endpoint estimates for the centered fractional maximal operator on the infinite rooted $k$-ary tree, $T$. Our first result is a Fefferman--Stein type estimate \[ w\bigl(\{M_\alpha f>\lambda\}\bigr) \lesssim_{\alpha,s} \frac1\lambda\sum_{x\in T}|f(x)|M_{\alpha,s}w(x), \qquad s>1, \] with a constant independent of the branching parameter $k$. Although the proof is inspired by the non-fractional argument of Ombrosi, Rivera-Ríos and Safe in \cite{ORRS}, the fractional normalization introduces a genuinely new scale interaction. At radius $r$, one must renormalize the function by $k^{r\alpha}$ while keeping the dyadic cutoff at $k^r$; this leads to a new summation mechanism involving simultaneously $k^{r\alpha}$ and $k^{r(1-\alpha)}$. We then study sharpness and the geometry of admissible weights. We construct counterexamples showing that the estimate cannot in general be reduced to $s=1$ for any $0<\alpha<1$. In the subcritical range $0<\alpha<1/2$, we characterize power-type radial weights satisfying the natural fractional $A_1$ condition and prove that the failure persists after any fixed finite number of iterations of $M_\alpha$. We also prove that $M_\alpha w\lesssim w$ is necessary for the corresponding weighted weak type estimate but is not sufficient in the subcritical range. Finally, we identify a phase transition: for $1/2\le\alpha<1$ no nonzero global weight can satisfy $M_\alpha w\lesssim w$.

## Regularity of Weak Solutions near Singular Minimizing Cones for the One-Phase Free Boundary Problem

Minyoung Jeong

对强可积且仅有孤立奇异性的1次齐次一相极小锥，建立相近非退化弱解的ε正则性。

Alt and Caffarelli proved an Allard-type $\epsilon$-regularity theorem near the flat one-homogeneous minimizer of the one-phase free boundary problem. A natural follow-up question is whether an analogous regularity result holds near singular minimizing cones. In this paper, we prove such an $\epsilon$-regularity theorem near a strongly integrable one-phase minimizing cone with an isolated singularity. More precisely, we show that, after a suitable translation and rotation, the free boundary of any non-degenerate weak solution sufficiently close to the cone is graphical over the free boundary of a leaf of the De Silva--Jerison--Shahgholian foliation. ## Reflexive polytopes can have doubly exponentially many vertices

Johannes Hofscheier、Benjamin Nill

研究d维反射格多胞体的顶点数上界。

Reflexive polytopes are lattice polytopes containing the origin in their interior such that also the dual polytope is a lattice polytope. On the basis of low-dimensional observations and of bounds known for special classes of reflexive polytopes, the second author conjectured in 2004 that the number of vertices of $d$-dimensional reflexive polytopes is bounded from above by $6^{d/2}$. In this note, we present counterexamples showing that this conjecture is false in every dimension $d \ge 8$.
