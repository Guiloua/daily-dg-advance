## A transition calculus for 0-pseudodifferential operators with applications to gas giant geometry

Andy Yin

中文解读待补齐

A gas giant Riemannian metric $g$ on a compact $n$-dimensional manifold-with-boundary $M$ blows up at the boundary $\partial M$ at the rate $1/x^\alpha$, for $\alpha \in (0,2)$, where $x \in C^\infty(M)$ is a boundary-defining function. Such metrics arise in the modeling of acoustic waves in gaseous planets. De Hoop-Ilmavirta-Kykkänen-Mazzeo [ arXiv:2403.05475 ] showed that, if $\alpha > 2/n$, the Laplace-Beltrami operator $\Delta_g$ is essentially self-adjoint and has discrete spectrum. Moreover, its spectrum is stable under truncations of $M$. The proof leverages the 0-calculus of Mazzeo-Melrose. We extend this theory by introducing a 0-transition calculus for studying families of 0-operators. We demonstrate a parametrix construction for $\Delta_g$ that is stable under truncations. Using this, we study the behavior of the resolvent of $\Delta_g$ under truncation and extend the characterization of the spectrum obtained by de Hoop-Ilmavirta-Kykkänen-Mazzeo. In particular, we prove that (on appropriate functional-analytic domains) simple eigenvalues admit asymptotic expansions in the truncation parameter $\epsilon$ and that the corresponding eigenfunctions admit joint asymptotic expansions in $(\epsilon, x)$.

## The Isoperimetric Flow and Synthetic Scalar Curvature for Causal Variational Principles

Felix Finster、Franz Gmeineder、Margarita Kraus

中文解读待补齐

An isoperimetric flow is introduced in the setting of causal variational principles. Describing the whole space by the support of a minimizing measure, we define an area functional on subsets by a surface layer integral. Mean curvature is defined via the first variation of this area functional. Using the method of minimizing movement, the isoperimetric flow is constructed as a Lipschitz curve of measures with prescribed volume change. As illustrating examples, we introduce and analyze ``smeared variants'' of the mean curvature flow in Euclidean space and on a Riemannian manifold. In these examples, it is shown that our area functional coincides with the usual area up to specified curvature corrections. As an application of our general results, by analyzing the volume and area of small isoperimetric balls, we introduce notions of local dimension and synthetic scalar curvature.

## On open weakly 2-semiconvex sets with nonempty 2-nonsemiconvexity-point sets in three-dimensional Euclidean space

Tetiana M. Osipchuk

中文解读待补齐

We study general topological properties of open weakly $2$-semiconvex sets in real three-dimensional Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^3$. An open set in $\mathbb{R}^3$ is called weakly $2$-semiconvex if through every boundary point of the set there passes a closed half-plane that does not intersect the set. The largest subset of the complement of a given set in $\mathbb{R}^3$ such that every closed half-plane intersecting this subset also intersects the given set is called the $2$-nonsemiconvexity-point set corresponding to the given one. 中文解读待补齐

The Poisson $\sigma$-model (PSM) over a closed worldsheet $\Sigma$ is reinterpreted as a theory of connections on a trivial principaloid bundle $P_0 = \Sigma \times G \to \Sigma$ with the symplectic structure groupoid $(G,\omega)$ integrating the Poisson target $(M,\Pi)$, and that of sections of the shadow bundle $F_0 = \Sigma \times M \to \Sigma$ - as defined in 2503.09886 [math.DG]. The flat directions of the PSM are explained as linearisations of automorphisms of $P_0$, modelled on maps from $\Sigma$ into the group of lagrangean bisections of $G$, which induce Poisson automorphisms of $(M,\Pi)$. The generalisation uses local product gerbes depending on local connections over trivialisations of $F$. The $G$-equivariant structure is proven to ensure a 2-stacky descent of the local gerbes to a global one over $F$. Its holonomy is taken as the definition of the generalised PSM. A cohomological discussion of obstructions against the descent is provided. Finally, a reformulation of the generalised PSM as a field theory with a topological gauge-symmetry defect is given, in which the $G$-equivariant structure is employed - alongside the transition cocycle of $P$ - as the datum of the defect.
