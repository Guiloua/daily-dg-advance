## Detecting Einstein metrics on products of spheres

Anna Siffert

在四类 S^(2p+1)×S^q 上构造无穷个非经典三半径余齐性 Einstein 度量，归一化 Ric=(2p+q)g。Ricci 平坦内区给出两个复振荡响应；第一通道消去共同相位，第二通道的相位不变符号由小比值与平衡族翻转。加权 C¹ 内外匹配把符号变换传回精确方程，任意大相位标签形成闭合解，奇异轨道曲率随尺度 ε⁻² 增长以分离等距类。

On \[ S^5\times S^2,\qquad S^5\times S^3,\qquad S^5\times S^4,\qquad S^7\times S^2, \] we construct infinitely many pairwise non-isometric real-analytic Einstein metrics. After discarding finitely many members, none is isometric or homothetic to a Riemannian product or to a classical two-summand Böhm metric. The metrics have the form \[ dt^2+a(t)^2g_{S^p}+b(t)^2g_{S^p}+c(t)^2g_{S^q}, \qquad p,q\ge2,\qquad 2p+q\le8. \] The construction starts with solutions for which the two non-collapsing radii at one singular orbit are very small. After magnifying this region, the Einstein equations approach a Ricci-flat problem whose solutions tend to a cone by a decaying oscillation. The main point is to determine exactly which part of this oscillation can still be seen by the closing conditions at the other end of the manifold. Rather than following the full, increasingly oscillatory shooting map, we extract two response quantities from the Ricci-flat limit. One determines the common oscillatory phase; after that phase has been removed, the second closing condition is reduced to a scalar sign change. A uniform inner--outer matching argument transfers this limiting sign change to the exact Einstein equations. As the initial scale tends to zero, the phase makes arbitrarily many turns, and the closing conditions are therefore met infinitely many times.

## Gradient Ricci Solitons on PTR-Submanifolds of Complex Space Forms

Jae Won Lee

用 Gauss–孤立子方程从 Hessian 算子与外在 Ricci 算子重构切向 Kähler 算子的平方，获得紧极小 PTR 孤立子的参数界、平凡性与刚性条件。

We study gradient Ricci solitons on partially totally real (PTR) submanifolds of complex space forms. In nonzero ambient holomorphic sectional curvature, the Gauss--soliton equation reconstructs the square $P^2$ of the tangential Kähler operator from the Hessian endomorphism and an extrinsic Ricci operator. Consequently, the PTR tangency spectrum is encoded by intrinsic soliton data together with the second fundamental form. For compact minimal PTR solitons with positive ambient curvature we obtain $\lambda\le c(n-1)/4$, with a strict version under a relative-nullity condition. We also prove compact triviality criteria, an exact trace-free anisotropy identity, a parallel-data rigidity result, and a nonexistence theorem for compact real hypersurfaces. A flat multi-level Gaussian model and a nonflat Segre $CR$-product provide explicit consistency checks and identify the limiting cases of the reconstruction formulas.

## A Quantitative Reeb-Form Obstruction for Real Hypersurfaces of Strict Nearly Kähler Six-Manifolds

Jae Won Lee、Chul Woo Lee

证明严格 nearly Kähler 六维流形的定向实超曲面上 dη 每点非零：若为零，形算子组合与接触算子既需交换又需反交换，造成代数矛盾。并推出指定 Yano 半对称度量连接的 Ricci 张量处处非对称。

Let $M^5$ be an oriented real hypersurface of a six-dimensional strict nearly Kähler manifold $(\widetilde M^6,\widetilde g,J)$, and let $\xi=-JN$ and $\eta=g(\,\cdot\,,\xi)$ denote the induced Reeb field and Reeb one-form. We prove the pointwise obstruction \[ (d\eta)_p\neq0\qquad\text{for every }p\in M. \] The proof is local and uses only the hypersurface identity \[ \nabla_X\xi=\phi AX-G(X,N) \] together with the six-dimensional strict nearly Kähler algebra. If $(d\eta)_p=0$, then \[ \phi A+A\phi=2G_N,\qquad G_NX=G(X,N), \] which forces the Hopf condition. On the contact hyperplane $\mathcal D_p=\ker\eta_p$, the endomorphism $\phi A+A\phi$ then commutes with $\phi$, whereas $G_N$ anti-commutes with $\phi$ and satisfies \[ G_N^2=-\alpha I_{\mathcal D_p},\qquad \alpha>0. \] This contradiction proves the obstruction. As a connection-theoretic consequence, for the standard Yano semi-symmetric metric connection associated with $\xi$, \[ \widehat\nabla_XY=\nabla_XY+\eta(Y)X-g(X,Y)\xi, \] one has in dimension five \[ \operatorname{Skew}\operatorname{Ric}^{\widehat\nabla}=-\frac32\,d\eta, \] and hence $\operatorname{Ric}^{\widehat\nabla}$ is nowhere symmetric. The obstruction also rules out any local induced almost contact class whose defining conditions include $d\eta=0$. We illustrate the result on the standard homogeneous strict nearly Kähler spaces.

## A counterexample to Yau's bounded-mean-curvature embedding question

Haoxuan Cheng

构造 R⁴ 上 0.9δ≤g≤1.1δ 的完整度量，使 Ricci 有界、全局单射半径正而 Rm 无界。用迹、散度和分量 Laplace 都消失的二次张量乘径向正则对数，分离线性化 Ricci 与全曲率，再拼接不交曲率峰。短测地线在小球的停留时长控制曲率积分，Jacobi 方程给单射半径；标量 Gauss 方程先以有界 H 和标量下界控制 |A|，再由完整 Gauss 方程排除有限维欧氏 C² 等距浸入。

Yau asked whether every complete Riemannian manifold with bounded Ricci curvature and positive injectivity radius admits an isometric embedding into some Euclidean space with bounded mean curvature. We construct a counterexample: a smooth complete metric on $\mathbb{R}^4$ with bounded Ricci curvature, positive injectivity radius, and unbounded full curvature. The Gauss equation then forces every $C^2$ isometric immersion of this metric into a finite-dimensional Euclidean space to have unbounded mean curvature. The metric is assembled from disjoint curvature peaks whose Ricci contractions stay uniformly bounded. A short-geodesic curvature estimate and the Jacobi equation give the uniform injectivity-radius bound. The construction works in every dimension at least four, and in dimension four the Ricci curvature may be chosen to tend to zero at infinity.

## A question of Ulam and a theorem of de Rham

Alexander Lytchak

对任意 proper 度量空间给出欧氏因子和非欧氏不可约因子的唯一带基点 ℓ² 分解，因子过固定点的纤维作为子集亦唯一，推出 A^n≅B^n⇒A≅B。论证把共同细化化为 1-Lipschitz 因子投影的交换，再在中心化平方距离生成的函数空间中转化为线性投影代数。一般完整空间还保留最大 Hilbert 因子和不含不可约因子的无原子余因子。

We generalize de Rham's decomposition theorem to proper metric spaces: every proper metric space has a unique decomposition as the product of a Euclidean space and a finite or countable pointed $\ell^2$-product of nontrivial irreducible non-Euclidean factors. More generally, every complete metric space splits canonically into a Hilbert factor, a pointed $\ell^2$-product of irreducible non-Euclidean factors, and a factor with no nontrivial irreducible factors. These results partially answer a question of Ulam.

## Minimality and Nonlinear Spectral Rigidity of Hypersurfaces in Real Space Forms

Zihao Wang

研究诱导度量下 J_X(Φ(τ)ν)=σΦ(τ)ν，其中 τ=nH。对正实解析 F，其对数导数 R_F 有正上下界并满足 R_F−tR_F′>0，作者证明 σ≥nc 时极小；p>1 的幂律均适用，p=2、σ=0 给欧氏及负常曲率双调和超曲面极小性。σ<nc 的非 CMC 分支有明确 H 界，非极小全脐例子表明无附加假设的谱边界尖锐；另对整个紧模型的 C⁴ 小法向图给模等距的 H⁴ 缺陷控制。

We study nonlinear mean-curvature spectral equations for hypersurfaces in Euclidean, spherical and hyperbolic space. For an admissible analytic response $F$ and ambient sectional curvature $c$, we prove minimality when the spectral parameter satisfies $\sigma\ge nc$, and strict mean-curvature bounds at gradient points when $\sigma<nc$. The class includes every power $F(t)=t^{p-1}$, $p>1$, and positive mixtures satisfying an explicit width condition. In particular, the result proves Chen's conjecture in Euclidean codimension one and biharmonic minimality in constant negative curvature, without completeness or holonomicity. A common real-analytic continuation and focal-trace argument treats all three geometries. Explicit umbilical models show that the unconditional spectral minimality range is sharp. Independently, we prove quantitative local rigidity, modulo ambient isometries, of compact nonminimal geodesic spheres in all three geometries and proper equal-radius Clifford hypersurfaces in spheres, for smooth laws with $F,F'>0$ near the model amplitude.

## Explicit Transgression Primitives on Flag Manifolds

Greg Weiler

以插值连接、非交换词求和与 Beta 积分构造旗流形上特征形式的相对 transgression 原始形式；说明高次数中曲率非交换项的结构，并以 Koszul DGA 恢复上同调多项式。

Borel's theorem presents the flag manifold cohomology as the coinvariant algebra $H^*(K/T; \mathbb{C}) \cong S(\mathfrak{h}^\vee)/I_W$, establishing the vanishing of characteristic forms $p_j(F_\mathfrak{t}) = \mathrm{tr}(F_\mathfrak{t}^j)$ non-constructively; projection from the Weil algebra $W(\mathfrak{k})$ yields closed primitives on $K$ failing to descend. We construct relative transgression primitives $\alpha_j \in (\Lambda^{2j-1}\mathfrak{m}^\vee)^T \cong {}^K\Omega^{2j-1}(K/T)$ satisfying $d_\mathfrak{m} \alpha_j = -p_j(F_\mathfrak{t})$ on $K/T$. Via connections $\theta_s = \theta_\mathfrak{t} + s\theta_\mathfrak{m}$, the curvature factors as $F_s = (1-s)(F_\mathfrak{t} - s\theta_\mathfrak{m}^2)$; expanding via non-commutative word sums and Euler's Beta integral yields a universal closed formula for all $j \ge 1$. These primitives exhibit a structural transition: $\alpha_2 = -\frac{1}{3}\mathrm{tr}(\theta_\mathfrak{m}^3)$ is curvature-free, $\alpha_3$ is linear in $F_\mathfrak{t}$, while at $j = 4$, $[F_\mathfrak{t}, \theta_\mathfrak{m}^2] \neq 0$ shatters cyclic trace symmetry into independent necklaces, producing the irreducible invariant $\mathrm{tr}(\theta_\mathfrak{m} \wedge F_\mathfrak{t} \wedge \theta_\mathfrak{m}^2 \wedge F_\mathfrak{t})$. On the $A_2$ root lattice ($\mathfrak{sl}_3(\mathbb{C})$), $T$-invariant cochains correspond to closed loops, $\alpha_2 = \Omega_- - \Omega_+$ is the oriented difference of triangles, and $d_\mathfrak{m}$ acts as an edge-fracturing operator witnessing the exactness of $c_2(E)$. While character theory encounters a multiplicity barrier at $k=4$ in Specht decompositions, a minimal Koszul DGA resolution $K_\bullet(p_2, \dots, p_k)$ resolves the coinvariant algebra, recovering the Poincaré polynomial $P_{K/T}(t) = \prod_{j=2}^k \frac{1-t^{2j}}{1-t^2}$ and Euler characteristic $\chi(K/T) = k!$ without matrix-rank ambiguities.

## Rectangular Cyclotomic Expansions of Colored HOMFLY-PT Polynomials

Honghuai Fang、Tian Zhou

对任意零 framing 结，证明固定矩形高度的宽度 Newton 展开系数属于整数 Laurent 环，带确定奇偶性与整除因子；再次按高度插值获得独立于宽、高的唯一双参数系数族，交换宽高对应 q↦q⁻¹。把一行分区设为 Newton 系数、其余分区系数置零，给出所引用矩形 cyclotomic 展开猜想的一种解；双扭结则另有含 Schur 特化及盒乘积核的显式有限公式。

We prove the rectangular cyclotomic expansion conjecture of Kameyama, Nawata, Tao, and Zhang for reduced colored HOMFLY-PT polynomials of arbitrary zero-framed knots. We also prove their explicit double-twist formula for every rectangular color and all pairs of nonzero integer twist parameters. For each knot, we construct a double Newton expansion with coefficients in $\mathbb Z[A^{\pm1},q^{\pm1}]$. These coefficients are uniquely determined and independent of the width and height of the rectangular color. The construction uses finite-rank representation symmetries and integral interpolation. The double-twist formula follows from a simultaneous exterior-power comparison of the two four-point Casimir operators and the known one-row formula. Finite transition formulas express the double Newton coefficients of double-twist knots in terms of the prescribed twist coefficients.

## Stratification of Joint Moduli Spaces of Higgs Bundles

Shiyu Cao

对 Teichmüller 空间上的多稳定 SL_r Higgs 丛联合模空间，用完整行列式一稳定子定义复 Whitney 分层；构造各层相对全纯辛形式及垂直 Poisson 括号，并描述超 Kähler 张量的平滑变化。

We study the marked analytic moduli space of polystable degree-zero $\mathrm{SL}_r(\C)$-Higgs bundles over Teichmüller space. The connected components of the orbit-type loci defined by the full determinant-one stabilizers form a complex Whitney stratification. The projection to Teichmüller space is a holomorphic submersion on every stratum. Each stratum carries a canonical relative holomorphic symplectic form. On each fiber, the Hitchin--Kobayashi correspondence is biholomorphic on every stratum and identifies its hyperkähler structure. Let $K$ be the trefoil or figure-eight knot and let $M_K$ denote zero surgery on $K$. Finally, we prove boundary-action, relative-homology, and local-smoothing obstructions to extending these local involutions across the gluing regions to a branched cover of the closed manifold.

## An exact hypergeometric Weierstrass representation for Smyth's free-boundary minimal disk in the regular tetrahedron

Steven Finch

为 Smyth 正四面体自由边界极小盘给出超几何 Weierstrass 数据、Gamma 值常数和精确全曲率；角参数固定，无 accessory 或自由参数，monodromy 稠密排除代数数据。

Smyth (1984) proved that every tetrahedron $T\subset\mathbb{R}^3$ contains three embedded minimal disks meeting $\partial T$ orthogonally, obtained as conjugate surfaces of Plateau solutions in a skew quadrilateral $Q$ whose edges are $c_i\nu_i$, where $\varphi_1,\varphi_2,\varphi_3,\varphi_4$ are faces of $T$, $c_i=\operatorname{Area}(\varphi_i)$ and $\nu_i$ are outward normal vectors. For the $\textit{regular}$ tetrahedron we give the Weierstrass data explicitly. The Gauss map is a Schwarz triangle function for a circular-arc triangle with angles $(\pi/4,\ \pi/2,\ \pi\beta/2)$, where $\beta=\arccos(-1/3)/\pi$; the hypergeometric parameters are linear in $\beta$ and there is $\textit{no accessory parameter and no free parameter}$. We record a closed form for $\kappa$ in $\Gamma$-values, a Wronskian closed form for $g'$, the exact total curvature $2\pi(2\beta-1)$, the symmetric-square structure of the data, and a rigidity statement: since $\beta/2$ is irrational, the projective monodromy is dense and the data $\textit{cannot}$ be algebraic. Numerically the representation extends to 28 digits -- $\operatorname{Area}/\ell^{2}=0.2172341554075574483760009126\ldots$ -- confirming a 2013 Surface Evolver computation of Ken Brakke.
