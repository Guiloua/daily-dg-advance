## Conformally Sasakian manifolds and the $k$-nullity condition

Fortuné Massamba、Pontsho Moile

在 Olszak 变换 (φ,e^σξ,e^(−σ)η,e^(−2σ)g) 下，由 dη 在 ker η 上的迹逐点决定 e^(−σ)，所以局部因子拼成全局因子且 Lee 形式 ω=dσ。若光滑函数 K 满足 R(X,Y)ξ=K{η(Y)X−η(X)Y}，则 K 在各连通分支为常数，包括三维。

We study almost contact metric manifolds that are locally conformal to Sasakian manifolds in the normalization of Olszak. We show that the local conformal factors are uniquely determined by the structure; consequently, every such manifold is globally conformal to a Sasakian manifold and its Lee form is exact. We then investigate the $k$-nullity condition $ R(X,Y)\xi=k\{\eta(Y)X-\eta(X)Y\}. $ We prove that a smooth nullity function is necessarily constant, including in dimension three. Moreover, the $k$-nullity condition is equivalent to the assertion that $f=\exp(\sigma)$ satisfies $ \mathrm{Hess}_g f=-(kf+C)g $ for a constant $C$, and hence to the fact that the conformal metrics $g$ and $g_0=\exp(-2\sigma)g$ have the same geodesic circles. We derive the range of the conformal factor, a local warped-product description, and a classification in the complete case: when the Lee form is nonzero, the complete metric is a simply connected space form of curvature $k$. Finally, we construct incomplete examples of nonconstant sectional curvature in every dimension $2n+1\geq 5$.

## Geometric Hammocks: Surface Laminations and the Ziegler Spectrum of String Algebras

Shantanu Sardar

作者借助带尖点双曲曲面的可容许测地线及 Chabauty 空间的走廊商，描述任意基数代数闭域上非 domestic 弦代数的纯内射模及 Ziegler 拓扑。

We give the first complete topological, geometric, and ergodic classification of the Ziegler spectrum $\Zg(\Lambda)$ of a non-domestic string algebra $\Lambda$ over an algebraically closed field of arbitrary cardinality, via a dictionary with Thurston's theory of geodesic laminations on a cusped hyperbolic surface. This is a model-theoretic counterpart of Haiden--Katzarkov--Kontsevich's realization (\emph{Publ.\ Math.\ IHES} 2017) of stability conditions on the bounded derived category of a gentle algebra via flat surfaces: pure-injective modules correspond to admissible geodesics, and the Ziegler topology to a corridor quotient of Chabauty space. Three consequences follow. The Krull--Gabriel dimension equals the surface's topological accumulation depth, and the string closure with all higher Cantor--Bendixson strata is globally homeomorphic to an explicit corridor space, with the pushout map to $\Zg(\Lambda)$ a homeomorphism if and only if $\Lambda$ is domestic. Prefix-free dyadic blocks in the geometric perfect core verify Puninski's cardinality-free two-chain criterion, resolving the open problem of superdecomposable pure-injectives over uncountable fields for all non-domestic string and special biserial algebras. Three purely algebraic invariants on the perfect core---localized pp-shape entropy, a $K_0$-weighted pressure function satisfying an algebraic Ruelle--Walters Variational Principle, and a pp-bifurcation index with a Gauss--Bonnet simplicity obstruction---strictly separate uncountable iso-entropic and iso-pressure families, and the inverse limit of the quotient pp-lattice recovers the transversal Cantor cross-section.

## On the Concavity of Rényi Entropy and Rényi Entropy Power along Heat Flows on Manifolds

Hao Wu、Lei Yu

对完备 n 维加权流形上的归一化 Witten 热流，定理3–5在相应分析假设与 Ric(L)≥0 下给 h_α 凹性，1≤α≤2。定理6–8的熵幂为 exp((β/m)h_α)，有限维 Ric_m,n(L)≥0 且 0<β≤2(2−α)，欧氏情形 m=n。α=2 时没有正 β 落在熵幂定理的范围内。

In this paper, we establish the concavity of Rényi entropy and Rényi entropy power along heat flow on Euclidean space of arbitrary dimension, for Rényi orders $\alpha\in[1,2]$. More generally, we prove the corresponding result along the heat flow generated by the Witten Laplacian on complete Riemannian manifolds under a suitable curvature-dimension condition. Our argument builds on novel functional inequalities that were recently developed for the study of Tsallis entropy concavity along the heat flow. We extend both these inequalities and the resulting concavity theorem from the Euclidean setting to the Riemannian manifold setting. In addition, we establish the complete monotonicity of Tsallis entropy of order $2$ along the heat flow and derive its spectral decomposition.

## Thompson indices of knots and the invertibility problem

Valeriano Aiello

用 Jones 多项式的跨度加 1 给 F 和有向 F 指标下界，用 HOMFLY–PT 次数给有向 F 和有向 F₃ 指标下界。通过树图穷举和针对性搜索计算至多 13 交叉素结的多项指标，所得数值由精确计算与区间算术认证。

Jones showed that Thompson's group $F$ produces all unoriented links, and introduced its oriented subgroup $\vec{F}$, which produces all oriented links; the same holds for the Brown-Thompson group $F_3$ and its oriented subgroup $\vec{F}_3$. The corresponding Thompson indices measure the complexity of a knot in these groups, much as the braid index does for braid groups. In this article we prove that the span of the Jones polynomial plus $1$ is a lower bound for the $F$- and $\vec{F}$-index, and we give lower bounds for the $\vec{F}$- and $\vec{F}_3$-index in terms of the degrees of the HOMFLY-PT polynomial. We then compute the four indices (the $F$-, $F_3$-, $\vec{F}$- and $\vec{F}_3$-index) for the prime knots with at most 13 crossings, by an exhaustive enumeration of tree diagrams (up to 13 leaves for $F$, 17 for $\vec{F}$, with the HOMFLY-PT polynomials of all the diagrams with 19 leaves, and 9 and 11 for $F_3$ and $\vec{F}_3$) completed by targeted searches; every value is rigorously certified, by exact computations and by verified computations of hyperbolic structures in interval arithmetic. The $F$-index is determined for all knots with at most 11 crossings, the $\vec{F}$-index for all knots with at most 8 crossings, and the $F_3$- and $\vec{F}_3$-index for all knots with at most 12 crossings. The main outcome of these computations is that the oriented indices are sensitive to the orientation: we find hundreds of non-invertible knots $K$ whose reverse $K^r$ has a different $\vec{F}$- or $\vec{F}_3$-index, the smallest being $8_{17}$ (with $\vec{F}_3$-index $7$ and $9$). The $\vec{F}$-index also distinguishes the Conway knot from its mutant, the Kinoshita-Terasaka knot.

## Every hyperbolic knot has an arc-faithful closed braid diagram

Haibin Hang

作者证明每个双曲结都承认弧忠实闭辫图。可在同一辫群中重写任意给定表示，使完备 holonomy 在每个交叉处的两个入射抛物不动点不同。

Every hyperbolic knot admits an arc-faithful closed braid diagram, answering Conjecture 29 of McPhail-Snyder. More precisely, any braid representing the knot can be reworded in the same braid group so that the complete holonomy has distinct incoming parabolic fixed points at every crossing. The construction replaces a pinched crossing by five non-pinched crossings when a suitable third strand is adjacent. On $n$ strands it uses at most $2n-1$ letters per replaced crossing. The resulting diagram realizes the complete holonomy by nondegenerate shapes in its four-term octahedral decomposition.
