## Delaunay Combinatorics of the Moduli and Teichmüller Space of Closed Genus-Two Riemann Surfaces

Armando Rodado Amaris

中文解读待补齐

Every closed Riemann surface of genus two is hyperelliptic, so it is determined by six branch points on the sphere, which become cone points of angle pi in the quotient orbifold metric. The Delaunay decomposition of these cone points, dual to the Voronoi diagram of the Weierstrass points, gives a trivalent graph with eight vertices, twelve edges and six faces. We classify these graphs. When the dual triangulation is simple there are exactly two types, dual to the octahedron and to a stacked triangulation, with orientation-preserving automorphism groups of orders 24 and 2, and we determine the Whitehead-move (diagonal-flip) orbit connecting them. Allowing bigons, the orbit closes at exactly twenty types. Iterated edge contraction from the two simple types gives a seven-level hierarchy of 2, 6, 22, 32, 22, 5 and 1 types, ending at two vertices joined by six parallel edges; closing under both contraction and expansion gives all 280 maps on the sphere with six vertices and no vertex of degree one. The rank of the linear cone-angle system of each type equals the real dimension 6 of the Teichmüller space. We realize both simple types by explicit hyperbolic cone spheres, in closed form for the octahedral type, build the corresponding genus-two surfaces by cutting and doubling, and show that the boundary x_e = 0 of the angle system is exactly the concyclicity of four branch points. The classification, the automorphism groups and the flip orbit are formally verified in Lean 4, and every enumeration is reproduced by an independent program, which also determines which types are realized by Delaunay decompositions.

## Determinants of amphichiral knots of braid index at most three

Werner Alois Stanggassinger

中文解读待补齐

We show that an odd number n is the determinant of an amphichiral knot of braid index at most three if and only if both n and n + 4 are sums of two squares. The proof rests on the known description of amphichiral knots of braid index three as closures of alternating 3-braid words with a rotation or reflection symmetry (Birman--Menasco, Stoimenov, Jung). Determinant formulas for closed 3-braids are also known (e.g. through the reduced Burau representation at t = -1); what is added here is their evaluation on the two symmetric families, which gives the condition on n + 4, and an explicit construction for the converse. For amphichiral knots of braid index three this describes which determinants occur, which bears on the determinant part of a question of Jung. We did not find the statement in the literature we searched. As a consequence, for an odd n with n + 4 not a sum of two squares, every amphichiral knot with determinant n has braid index at least five; we test this on 4,999 amphichiral knots of Burton's census with up to 19 crossings whose determinant satisfies this hypothesis.

## Invariant measures with uniform projection on surfaces and billiards

Xiaolong Han

中文解读待补齐

In this paper, we study probability measures on the unit tangent bundle which are invariant under the geodesic flow and whose projection to the base manifold is the normalized Riemannian volume. We show that the Liouville measure is the only such measure on a complete oriented surface of finite area with ergodic geodesic flow. In particular, this resolves a conjecture of Katok--Knieper--Weiss in dimension two. The same holds for ergodic planar billiards with piecewise C2 boundary, such as the Bunimovich stadium, the Sinai billiard, and generic polygons. The proof combines the Fourier analysis in the fibers with holomorphic functions on the transport twistor space. As a consequence, on these surfaces and on the Bunimovich stadium, a high-energy sequence of normalized Laplacian eigenfunctions tends to be equidistributed in the phase space if and only if it tends to be equidistributed in the physical space. In particular, on every stadium, every failure of equidistribution in the phase space implies scarring in the physical space. Combined with Hassell's theorem, this gives a new proof of scarring in the physical space for almost every stadium. In higher dimensions, we obtain an entropy bound for the ergodic components of such measures. We also construct counterexamples for hyperbolic toral automorphisms.

## Level Set Method; partial regularity in ${\mathbf R}^3$

Tobias Holck Colding、William P. Minicozzi II

中文解读待补齐

In the level set method, all level sets of a function move by mean curvature. The function is then a solution of a degenerate parabolic equation, the level set equation. Since the beginning of the 1990s it has been known that solutions are Lipschitz and, in general, not $C^2$. We show that, along a level set that is initially a smooth closed surface in ${\mathbf R}^3$, the Hessian of the solution is bounded up to its first singular time. In fact, at each non-generic singularity the Hessian tends to zero, at a rate given by its instability. Using these bounds, we show in the sequel that, surprisingly, at the singular time the solution is twice differentiable and has a backward time derivative at every point of the level set, whatever the singularity, and identify both the Hessian and time derivative.

## Link Spectra of Lorenz-like Templates and the Horseshoe

Thiago de Paiva、Alexander He

中文解读待补齐

We give a complete classification of the inclusion relations between the link spectrum of the horseshoe template $L(0,1)$ and those of the nonnegative Lorenz-like templates $L(0,a)$. For a template $T$, its link spectrum $\mathscr L(T)$ consists of the link types carried by $T$, allowing parallel strands along its strips. For integers $a\geq0$, the spectra of $L(0,a)$ and $L(0,1)$ are comparable under inclusion if and only if $a\notin\{0,2\}$. More precisely, \[ \mathscr L(L(0,a))=\mathscr L(L(0,1)) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a=1, \] while \[ L(0,a)\prec L(0,1) \qquad (a\geq3). \] For $a=0$ and $a=2$, the two spectra are incomparable. The main new containment is \[ L(0,2n)\preceq L(0,1)\qquad(n\geq2), \] which goes beyond the classical parity-preserving nesting relations. A key ingredient is a braid-theoretic criterion: every $T$-link \[ T((r_1,s_1),\ldots,(r_k,s_k)) \] with $s_k\geq2r_k$ is a horseshoe link. This gives a large explicit family belonging to both the Lorenz and horseshoe spectra. In the reverse direction, we construct a horseshoe link which is not Lorenz, thereby disproving the Birman--Kofman conjecture that every horseshoe link is a Lorenz link. For each integer $m\geq6$, we obtain infinitely many horseshoe links of braid index $m$ excluded from every other nonnegative $L(0,a)$. These examples give infinitely many links with any prescribed number of components at least three. In the other direction, for each positive number of components, we give infinitely many links carried by both $L(0,0)$ and $L(0,2)$ and excluded from the horseshoe spectrum.
