## A Homotopical Geometry of the Multinomial and Negative-Multinomial Potentials

Shintaro Yoshizawa

对多项分布与负多项分布的对数配分函数作凸组合，证明 Hessian 正定并描述二维曲率变号；构造相应复合分布与 Bregman 投影恒等式。

We study the one-parameter family of Hessian metrics $g_\alpha=\alpha h_++(1-\alpha)h_-$, $\alpha\in[0,1]$, formed by convexly combining the log-partition functions (not the densities) of the multinomial and negative multinomial Fisher information Hessians. Four results are proved in full. (1) $g_\alpha\succ0$ for all $\alpha\in[0,1]$; its Gaussian curvature ($n=2$) is $-\tfrac14$ at $\alpha=0$ and $+\tfrac14$ at $\alpha=1$, but non-constant for intermediate $\alpha$, changing sign at a closed-form critical value $\alpha^\ast(w)$, with an analytic uniqueness proof. (2) Viewing $g_\alpha$ as the spatial metric of a Kasner-type volume equation, positive-definiteness forces one conserved quantity $\beta\le0$, while a second, independent quantity $C=4\det K$ governs the equation's branch (bounded oscillatory, parabolic, or unbounded growth) and is not fixed by $\beta$ or positive-definiteness alone; we show this discriminant dictionary is a theorem about the abstract matrix equation, to which $g_\alpha$ supplies initial data of either sign of $C$ without the trajectories remaining on $\{g_\alpha(\theta)\}$ itself. (3) $\Psi_\alpha$ is identified as the log-partition function of a compound distribution: a negative-binomial latent count $M$ (parameter $r=1-\alpha$), augmented by a Bernoulli parity bit and split multinomially, with non-negative base measure for every $\alpha$. (4) The associated Bregman divergence obeys a generalised Pythagorean theorem whose projection onto the symmetric locus is the arithmetic mean, independent of $\alpha$; the endpoints' curvature sign matches the Gauss--Bonnet excess of genuine geodesic triangles. Numerical checks are kept separate from analytic proofs throughout.

## The Second Gap for Minimal Hypersurfaces in the Unit Sphere with Constant Cubic Trace

Shanlin Guan

在闭定向、常 |h|² 与常 tr(h³) 的球面极小超曲面上，以二次缺陷张量及无散度三次多项式的积分估计排除 n<S<2n。等号推出 tr(h³)=0、h³=3h，各连通分支局部为 Cartan 三主曲率模型。

Let $M^n$, $n\ge3$, be a closed oriented minimal hypersurface in the unit sphere with constant scalar curvature and constant $f_3=\tr(h^3)$, where $h$ is the second fundamental form. Equality holds if and only if each connected component is locally congruent to a Cartan minimal isoparametric hypersurface with three distinct principal curvatures.

## Asymptotic dimension of commutative monoid actions

Jinmin Wang、Jing Yu、Jingming Zhu

We prove that every bounded-to-one action of a finitely generated commutative monoid has asymptotic dimension at most the torsion-free rank of its group completion, with control uniform for a fixed monoid, generating set, and generator fiber bound. ## A Model for the space of convex quadrilaterals

Carl Eberhart

构造每个凸四边形相似类恰有一个代表的模型，并以四维胞腔参数化；描述梯形子集对该模型的分割。

This paper describes a 'model' for the space of convex quadrilaterals, that is, a set $\mathbb{Q}$ of convex quadrilaterals in the plane which gives a 'cross-section' of the equivalence classes of similar convex quadrilaterals, in the sense that every convex quadrilateral is similar to exactly one member of $\mathbb{Q}$. It is also has the property that quadrilaterals which have nearly the same vertices are close to each other in the model in the Hausdorff metric. We also parameterize our model with the 4-cell and use that to describe and investigate various classes of quadrilaterals. For example, we show that the trapezoids form a 3-dimensional closed subset of the model which separates the model into 3 disjoint open sets, each homeomorphic with a 4-cell. ## Hopf Reduction and Multiplicity-Resolved Endpoints for the Classical Willmore Flow of Hopf Tori

Mohameden Ahmedou、Ruben Jakob

利用拉回圆丛归一化建立 Hopf 环面经典 Willmore 流与球面弹性流的参数化对应，时间尺度为 τ=4t。结合既有曲线流全收敛，得到表面模重参数化的光滑全收敛，并定量控制法向 L² 长度、面积测度及极限覆盖重数。

We establish an exact parametrized reduction of the classical Willmore flow of Hopf tori in the round three-sphere to the spherical elastic flow. In Willmore-flow time the reduction is $(\partial_t\gamma)^{\perp}=-4\nabla_{L^2}\mathcal E(\gamma)$; its global reconstruction uses a pullback-circle-bundle normalization and does not require preservation of simplicity along flow lines. Combining the known global subconvergence of the spherical elastic flow with Pozzetta's full convergence theorem then yields full smooth convergence of flow lines, modulo domain diffeomorphisms, for every simply parametrized Hopf torus at arbitrary initial energy. We then obtain quantitative conclusions for the unreparametrized trajectory: finite normal $L^2$-metric length, total-variation convergence of the pullback area measures, bounded-Lipschitz convergence of the image measures, and explicit tail estimates. The density of the full varifold endpoint equals the covering multiplicity of the limiting elastic profile, and Pinkall's marked flat lattices converge. In particular, a simple initial Hopf immersion with energy arbitrarily close to $4\pi^2$ from above can smoothly converge to some parametrization of the Clifford torus with multiplicity two, although its limiting conformal class in moduli space is exactly the square class.

## Recovering complex structure from $(n,0)$-form with implications for Vafa-Witten equation

Zhengxiong Cao

研究从类似标量 (n,0)-形式的丛值闭或余闭形式恢复 Z₂-复结构；在四维及四元 Kähler 背景延拓过零集，一般情形仅局部成立。

Given an even-dimensional smooth manifold and a (co)(closed) bundle-valued $n$-form $a$ that "resembles" a scalar $(n,0)$-form, we may (or may not) recover a "$\mathbb{Z}_2$-(almost) complex structure" under certain assumptions. In the case of a $4$-manifold or a quaternion-Kähler manifold, we can recover a compatible $\mathbb{Z}_2$-complex structure on the whole manifold including the zero locus of $a$. In general, we can recover a $\mathbb{Z}_2$-(almost) complex structure wherever $a$ satisfies a certain nondegeneracy condition, but not necessarily on the whole manifold, with a counterexample identified. The above condition of "resembling" a scalar $(2,0)$-form is satisfied by any $\mathrm{U}(1)$-invariant solution to the Vafa-Witten equation on a compact Kähler surface. This hints at a possibility to generalise the notion of $\mathrm{U}(1)$-invariant solutions to oriented Riemannian $4$-manifolds. However, our results above imply any solution satisfying the condition, and thus seemingly any potential "generalised $\mathrm{U}(1)$-invariant solution", must be trivial if the $4$-manifold admits no compatible $\mathbb{Z}_2$-complex structure.

## Topological Obstructions to Riemannian-Lorentzian Continuation

Nathalie E. Rieger、Ödül Tetik

对沿超曲面领口拼接的流形，以相对 Euler 类消失刻画从 Riemannian 区域到 Lorentzian 区域的横截光滑符号变换，并构造允许紧致类型的例子。

Motivated by problems in instanton continuation, we study geometro-topological selection rules for continuing a Riemannian region into a Lorentzian region through a smooth transverse change of signature. Given a collar-gluing $M=\overline{M_R}\cup_{\mathcal{H}}\overline{M_L}$ of a smooth manifold $M$ along a hypersurface $\mathcal{H}$, we show that a metric which is Riemannian on $M_R$ and Lorentzian on $M_L$ exists if and only if a relative Euler class obstruction on $\overline{M_L}$ vanishes. This obstruction combines ordinary Euler characteristics $\chi$ in a manner that depends on which boundary components of the Riemannian region represent big bangs and which represent big crunches. It also restricts the choice of $M_R$; e.g., $\chi(\overline{M_R})=\chi(M)$ and $\chi(\overline{M_R})=\chi(\overline{M_L})$ are required when $\dim M$ is even or odd, respectively. We also determine which compactness types of $\overline{M_R}$, $\mathcal{H}$, and $\overline{M_L}$ can occur in such a collar-gluing, and construct explicit signature-changing metrics for each case. Topology change can be entirely contained within the Lorentzian region when $M$ is the interior of a compact manifold with boundary.

## A Positive Mass Type Theorem for a Singular Toroidal Static Model

Zhixin Wang

对满足 Condition C 的三维不可约、非负标量曲率流形，比较奇异内端与无穷远端的平坦环面面积，证明 |h₁|≥|h₂|；等号恢复静态模型 dr²+r^(4/3)h。再以 Shi–Tam 型构造得到平坦环面边界不等式。

We study positive mass and related Brown--York type inequalities for three-dimensional manifolds modeled on a static space with flat toroidal slices.
