## On the minimality of the compact right-angled 120-cell

Lizi Guo、Fangting Zheng

We prove that a compact right-angled polytope in hyperbolic 4-space has at least 120 facets, with equality only for the right-angled 120-cell. This answers a question of Potyagailo and Vinberg. As a consequence, the right-angled 120-cell is the unique compact right-angled 4-polytope of minimal volume and f-vector.

## Space curves in Hahn calculus: rotation structure, a fundamental theorem, and $q$-deformed invariants at the fixed point

Umut Selvi、Derya Sağlam

针对收缩 σ(t)=qt+ω 的 Hahn 差分微积分，作者将空间曲线的 Frenet 标架沿轨道的演化描述为转角与挠角的旋转，建立平面性判别和曲线基本定理。固定点处曲率、挠率分别出现 [2]_q/2、[3]_q/3 采样因子；边长归一化可消去各自因子，但其商仍保留在渐近角度比中。

We develop the differential geometry of space curves in Hahn's quantum calculus, where the derivative is the difference quotient along the contraction $\sigma(t)=qt+\omega$ with fixed point $\omega_0=\omega/(1-q)$. The Hahn Frenet frame is transported along every orbit of $\sigma$ by rotations through turning and torsion angles, which identifies it with the discrete Frenet frame of the inscribed orbit polygon. An embedded regular Hahn curve is determined, up to linear reparametrization about $\omega_0$, by its trace and the induced contraction. We prove a sharp planarity criterion and a fundamental theorem via an infinite product of rotations and the Jackson--Nörlund integral. For smooth curves, expansions uniform in $q$ show that at $\omega_0$ the Hahn curvature and torsion carry the factors $[2]_q/2$ and $[3]_q/3$. These factors, a sampling effect of geometrically decaying steps, disappear under edge-length normalization, but their quotient persists in the limiting ratio of torsion and turning angles of every polygon inscribed in a smooth curve with monotonically converging vertices and edge lengths decaying with ratio~$q$. In uniformly sampled relaxation towards a stable node, the factors can become measurable biases. We characterize generalized Hahn helices by a two-step relation between turning and torsion angles; their Lancret ratio at $\omega_0$ is $\pm([2]_q/2)\cot\vartheta$, where $\vartheta$ is the helix angle. Unlike the classical case, a constant ratio of torsion and curvature does not characterize helices: curves with constant curvature and torsion are never generalized Hahn helices, and, near $\omega_0$, unit-speed helices with a given ratio are unique up to similarities and linear reparametrization.

## Absorption probabilities of random walks without symmetry or general position

Artyom Bolotin、Dmitry Zaporozhets

对 R^d 中具有独立同分布增量的随机游走，作者将前 n 个部分和凸包不含原点的概率，表达为按 n 的有序正整数分拆加权的独立游走终点凸包不含原点概率之和；不要求对称性、一般位置或矩条件。结果来自任意向量的确定性排列恒等式，并统一若干吸收概率与生成函数公式。

Let $S_1,S_2,\dots$ be a random walk in $\mathbb R^d$ with independent identically distributed increments. We show that, for every distribution of the increments and for every $n\ge1$, \[ \mathbb P\bigl[0\notin\mathrm{conv}(S_1,\dots,S_n)\bigr] = \sum_{m=1}^n\frac1{m!}\sum_{\substack{k_1,\dots,k_m\ge1\\ k_1+\dots+k_m=n}}\frac{1}{k_1\cdots k_m}\,\mathbb P\bigl[0\notin\mathrm{conv}(S^{(1)}_{k_1},\dots,S^{(m)}_{k_m})\bigr], \] where $S^{(1)},S^{(2)},\dots$ are independent copies of the walk. No symmetry, general position or moment assumptions are needed. The result rests on a deterministic identity: for arbitrary vectors $x_1,\dots,x_n$, the number of orderings for which the convex hull of the partial sums avoids the origin equals the number of permutations for which the convex hull of the cycle sums avoids it. We derive it from a permutation-averaging identity for valuations obtained in our earlier work. Consequences include the identities of Sparre Andersen and Spitzer, the distribution-free absorption probabilities of Kabluchko, Vysotsky and Zaporozhets, their generating function found by Petrov, Randon-Furling and Zaporozhets, and a formula for the probability that the origin is never absorbed.

## Rigidity and deformations of Besse families of minimal hypersurfaces

Christian Lange

作者把 Zoll 超曲面族推广为允许非嵌入及例外叶的 Besse 族，并以双 Legendrian 纤维化描述其接触几何。球面除 S^4 外的 Besse 族每个超曲面均正则；偶维球面除 S^4 外还均嵌入。另一方面，任意奇维至少三的情形存在非恒曲率度量，承载含非嵌入极小超曲面的 Besse 族。

We generalize Zoll families of hypersurfaces to Besse families of hypersurfaces, which are defined differential-topologically and may contain non-embedded and exceptional hypersurfaces. A contact-geometric discussion presents them as double Legendrian fibrations and shows that regular hypersurfaces are either spheres or real projective spaces. In analogy with the Berger conjecture on simply connected manifolds all of whose geodesics are closed, we show that every hypersurface of a Besse family on a sphere other than the 4-sphere is regular. On even-dimensional spheres other than the 4-sphere we obtain a stronger conclusion, generalizing a theorem of Gromoll and Grove about 2-spheres all of whose geodesics are closed: every hypersurface of a Besse family is regular and embedded. Moreover, building on work by Ambrozio and Guajardo, we provide metrics of non-constant curvature carrying Besse families of non-embedded minimal hypersurfaces in every odd dimension at least three, and Besse families of minimal hypersurfaces with exceptional hypersurfaces on orbifolds in every dimension at least two. We also give a short proof that manifolds admitting Zoll families of embedded hypersurfaces are homeomorphic to spheres or real projective spaces, recovering a recent result of Martins up to homeomorphism.

## On the Upsilon invariant and the Baldwin-Vela-Vick b-invariant of fibered knots

Linh Truong

对有理同调三维球中的零同调纤维结 K，作者证明：若某个 t∈[0,2/(n+1)) 处 Upsilon 的斜率为 −g(K)，则 Baldwin–Vela-Vick 不变量 b(K)>n；并证明 Alexander 分次 g(K)−b(K) 处结 Floer 同调非零，给出 b 任意大的纤维缆结族。

For a null-homologous fibered knot $K$ in a rational homology sphere, Baldwin and Vela-Vick define a refinement $b(K)$ of the Ozsváth-Szabó contact invariant. We prove that if the Ozsváth-Stipsicz-Szabó concordance invariant Upsilon has slope $\Upsilon'_K(t)=-g(K)$ for some $t\in[0,\frac{2}{n+1})$, where $g(K)$ is the genus of $K$, then $b(K)>n$. This extends a result of He, Hubbard, and the author on the Upsilon invariant and right-veering monodromies. We also prove that the knot Floer homology of a fibered knot in the $g(K)-b(K)$ Alexander grading is nonvanishing. As an application, we give many families of fibered cable knots whose $b(K)$ invariant grows arbitrarily large. Finally, we prove that if a monodromy of a fibered knot is neither right-veering nor left-veering, then the third-to-top term of knot Floer homology is nonvanishing.

## The Number of Polytope Trees and Beyond

Ali Aliev、Natalia Amburg

研究把正多胞体副本粘合形成的复形的对称加权计数。对多胞体树以及附着在固定初始复形上的树，作者给出 cut-and-join 递推、生成函数微分方程、显式计数及渐近估计；随后将方法扩展到对偶图有圈的复形，按多胞体数和自由面数建立二元生成函数。

We study the symmetry-weighted enumeration of complexes formed by gluing copies of a regular polytope. For polytope trees and complexes obtained by attaching such trees to a fixed initial complex, we derive cut-and-join recurrences, differential equations for generating functions, explicit counting formulas, and asymptotic estimates. We then extend the method to complexes with cycles in their dual graphs, enumerating them by the numbers of polytopes and free facets. We derive recurrence relations and partial differential equations for the corresponding bivariate generating functions. In this setting, we obtain recurrences for connected configurations and explicit coefficient formulas for possibly disconnected configurations, both with and without a distinguished initial complex.

## Volume of area-bounded PGL3-Hitchin moduli space

Qiongling Li、Beibei Liu、Weixu Su、Zhe Sun

## Blow-up analysis and compactness for critical Dirac equations on $\mathbb S^n$

Ting Li、Zhongwei Tang

在圆球 Sn、n≥3 的临界 Dirac 方程上，定理 1.1 假设 Hi 一致正、C0 收敛，且临界质量 limsup 不超过 Hmax^(-n)(n/2)^n|Sn|，得到发散序列唯一爆破点位于 H 全局极大值及单个基态泡耗尽质量。局部统一 C1 控制进一步给出孤立简单爆破；C2 条件与 Pohozaev 恒等式导出 Hess H(p)=0。

We study blow-up and compactness for the critical nonlinear Dirac equation \[ D_{g_0}\psi_i = H_i|\psi_i|^{\frac{2}{n-1}}\psi_i \qquad \text{on }\mathbb S^n,\quad n\ge 3, \] where $g_0$ is the round metric and $H_i\to H>0$ in $C^0(\mathbb S^n)$. 在 Hⁿ⁺¹、n≥3 中研究保持 quermassintegral、由 (Eₖ/Eℓ)^(1/(k−ℓ)) 驱动的曲率商流。对 1≤ℓ<k 且 k+ℓ≤n，证明正截面曲率保持；从光滑闭严格凸且截面曲率为正的超曲面出发，流长期存在并光滑指数收敛到测地球。进一步对边界截面曲率非负的光滑有界凸域，在 0≤ℓ<k≤n、k+ℓ≤n 范围建立 Wₖ 与 Wℓ 的尖锐 Alexandrov–Fenchel 不等式，等号仅为测地球。

We study quermassintegral-preserving curvature flows in $\mathbb H^{n+1}$, $n\geq3$, driven by the curvature quotient $(E_k/E_\ell)^{1/(k-\ell)}$. For $1\leq\ell<k$ with $k+\ell\leq n$, we prove that positive sectional curvature is preserved. Starting from a smooth closed strictly convex hypersurface with positive sectional curvature, the flow exists for all time and converges smoothly and exponentially to a geodesic sphere. As an application, we establish sharp Alexandrov--Fenchel inequalities between $W_k$ and $W_\ell$ for smooth bounded convex domains whose boundaries have nonnegative sectional curvature, whenever $0\leq\ell<k\leq n$ and $k+\ell\leq n$. Equality holds if and only if the domain is a geodesic ball. Together with previously known cases, these inequalities give the full family of Alexandrov--Fenchel inequalities for this class of domains in $\mathbb H^6$. We also construct counterexamples showing that $k+\ell\leq n$ is the optimal range for preservation of positive sectional curvature by these flows.

## Morse index characterization of the Veronese projective plane

Alessandro Carlotto、Yangyang Li、Zhihan Wang

对 S⁴ 中闭、连通、嵌入且非全测地的极小曲面，作者刻画 Simons 指标下界 5 的等号：Morse 指标恰为 5 当且仅当曲面全等于 Veronese 射影平面。结合 Bernstein–Ketover 的近期进展，作者称由此解决四维中法丛 Euler 数非零曲面的 Willmore 极小化问题的 Kusner 1996 年猜想。

Following Simons' seminal work in 1968, every closed, connected, embedded minimal surface in the four-sphere that is not totally geodesic has Morse index at least five. In this paper, we prove that equality holds if and only if the surface is congruent to the Veronese projective plane. Relying on recent advances by Bernstein and Ketover, this characterization settles Kusner's 1996 conjecture on the minimizers of the Willmore functional in dimension four for surfaces whose normal bundle has nonzero Euler number. ## Characteristic gluing to extremal Kerr horizons and the third law of black hole thermodynamics in vacuum

Stefan Czimek

作者声称建立非微扰、非轴对称的特征拼接：Kerr 黑洞外通信域中任一 Boyer–Lindquist 球面可沿单条向外零超曲面，精确拼接到质量足够大的极端 Kerr 视界球面，并匹配包含横向曲率的完整球面数据。应用构造单端渐近平坦真空初值，其最大演化含精确 Schwarzschild 表观视界及较晚先进时间的精确极端 Kerr 事件视界。

We prove a non-perturbative, non-axisymmetric characteristic gluing theorem for the Einstein vacuum equations: every Boyer-Lindquist sphere in the domain of outer communication of a Kerr black hole, in particular every symmetry sphere of a Schwarzschild exterior, can be glued along a single outgoing null hypersurface to a horizon sphere of an extremal Kerr black hole of sufficiently large mass. The gluing is exact at both ends and matches the full sphere data, including the transversal curvature. This proves a conjecture of Kehle and Unger in the extremal case. We first raise the angular momentum to the extremal value by rotating shear pulses of non-axisymmetric gravitational radiation, and then damp the incoming data exponentially along a long non-expanding neck. At the degenerate target horizon we invert the linearised problem at all angular frequencies, using a factorisation of a fourth-order angular operator into an angular Teukolsky-Starobinsky product. As an application, we construct one-ended asymptotically flat vacuum initial data whose maximal development contains an exactly Schwarzschild apparent horizon and, at a later advanced time, an exactly extremal Kerr event horizon. This is the first rigorous counterexample to the third law of black hole thermodynamics in vacuum, and proves a second conjecture of Kehle and Unger.

We construct $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Yang-Mills-Higgs fields on a broad class of noncompact Riemannian manifolds, concentrating in the large-mass limit along prescribed nondegenerate minimal submanifolds of codimension three.
