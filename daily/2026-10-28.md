## Observable diameter of product measures with a power-type interior zero

Takashi Shioya

研究 [0,1] 上概率测度的 ℓ^p 乘积空间：密度在一个内部点具有幂次型零点，且零点两侧允许不同消失阶。作者对所有 1≤p≤∞ 给出可观测直径的尖锐双边估计，证明随乘积维数变化的渐近阶由两侧较大的消失阶决定；p=2 时相变恰发生在谱隙阈值。

We determine the asymptotic order of the observable diameter of $\ell^p$-product spaces associated with a probability measure on $[0,1]$ whose density has a power-type interior zero. Different vanishing orders are allowed on the two sides of the zero. For every $1 \le p \le \infty$, we obtain sharp two-sided estimates and show that the asymptotic order is governed by the larger of the two vanishing orders. In the case $p=2$, the transition occurs exactly at the threshold for the spectral gap.

## Formal Neighborhood Equivariant Cohomology and Kirwan Surjectivity at Singular Values

Zhenxi Huang

对紧 Lie 群的 Hamilton 作用，作者用切片群胚的 Cartan 层及奇异能级商的闭嵌入定义形式邻域等变上同调，并构造形式 Kirwan 映射。在切片能级满足局部等变同伦回缩条件时证明满射；最弱表述为每个轨道附近存在指定 Cartan 复形与能级支撑复形的局部拟同构。

Let \(G\) be a compact Lie group acting Hamiltonianly on a symplectic manifold \(M\) with moment map \(\mu:M\to\g^*\). For a singular value \(a\) of \(\mu\), the symplectic quotient \(\mu^{-1}(a)/G\) is stratified and the classical Kirwan map is not defined. We introduce a \emph{formal neighborhood equivariant cohomology} \(\cH_G^*(a)\) using the slice-groupoid Cartan sheaf and the closed embedding \(\mu^{-1}(a)/G\hookrightarrow \mu^{-1}(U_a)/G\) for a \(G\)-invariant neighborhood \(U_a\) of \(a\). We define a formal Kirwan map \[ \kappa_{\mathrm{formal}}:H_G^*(M)\longrightarrow \cH_G^*(a). \] We prove that \(\kappa_{\mathrm{formal}}\) is surjective under a local \emph{equivariant homotopy retraction} condition on the slice level sets \[ C_x=\mu_x^{-1}(a|_{\g_x})\subset S_x, \] which is satisfied, for example, when \(a\) is a regular value of every slice moment map, or when \(a=0\) and \(C_x\) is a \(G_x\)-equivariant cone. The weakest condition we identify is the existence, for each \(x\in\mu^{-1}(a)\), of a local quasi-isomorphism \[ \Car(S_x,G_x)\simeq \cC^\bullet_{C_x} \] in a \(G_x\)-equivariant neighborhood of the orbit, without requiring a global tubular neighborhood. As an application we recover, in the formal framework, the even-degree surjectivity theorem of Delarue--Ramacher--Schmitt for \(S^1\)-actions and clarify why odd-degree surjectivity fails in general.

## Rigidity and Affine Deformations of Foliations on Nilpotent and Solvable Contact Lie Algebras

Cheikh Khoule、Rafik Khalfi

对 2n+1 维 Lie 代数上的接触形式 β 和非零可积一形式 α，作者研究仿射路径 (1−t)α+tβ 何时成为与 β 同向的接触形式。得到由相互作用顶形式决定标量的非负判据；幺模情形标量为 (n+1)α(Zβ)，非幺模情形另含模特征项。

Let g be a Lie algebra of dimension 2n+1, let beta be a contact form, and let alpha be a nonzero integrable 1-form. We study when the affine path alpha_t = (1-t)alpha + t beta consists of contact forms with the orientation determined by beta \wedge (d beta)^n. We first establish an algebraic criterion: affine deformability is equivalent to the non-negativity of a scalar obtained from the interaction form Theta(alpha, beta) = alpha \wedge (d beta)^n + n beta \wedge d alpha \wedge (d beta)^{n-1}. For unimodular Lie algebras this scalar is (n+1)alpha(Z_beta), where Z_beta is the Reeb vector. We also derive a formula involving the modular character tau(X) = tr(ad_X) in the non-unimodular case. On nilpotent contact Lie algebras, every integrable 1-form is closed; using the standard structural properties of nilpotent contact Lie algebras, this implies that every left-invariant foliation is an affine origin for every fixed contact form. An explicit three-dimensional example illustrates that the modular term can yield affine deformability even when alpha(Z_beta) = 0. We discuss the implications for left-invariant forms on Lie groups and compact quotients. We do not claim a general obstruction example in the non-unimodular case; establishing such an example requires a separate verified construction.

## Counting Markov triples and simple geodesics

Morten S. Risager

作者给出最大分量小于 x 的 Markov 三元组计数渐近式，将误差改进为 o(log x)。证明利用 Markov 三元组与模穿孔环面上简单闭测地线的对应，并精细分析 McShane–Rivin 单位球；还由 Riemann ζ 函数经典零点自由区得到显式误差，在准 Riemann 假设下进一步改进。

We give an asymptotic formula for the number of Markov triples with largest entry below $x$. The proof uses the correspondence between Markov triples and simple closed geodesics on the modular punctured torus and a careful analysis of the McShane-Rivin unit ball.

Ha Tuan Dung、Yi Li

In the stationary reversible setting, our results specialize to the Witten Laplacian and recover the unweighted Cai-Wang estimates \cite{Cai}.

## Contact topology and intrinsic characterization of transversely vanishing curl eigenforms

Robert Cardona、Willi Kepplinger

研究三维流形上横截消失的 curl 特征一形式，以 near-contact 形式为框架，为其核分布建立 Gray 型稳定性及奇点邻域定理。由此得到 curl 特征形式的接触拓扑稳定性、按形式同伦资料的 near-contact 分类，并从 curl 谱理论给出闭可定向三维流形存在接触结构的另一证明。

We study the geometry and topology of transversely vanishing eigenforms of the curl operator on $3$-manifolds, placing them in the broader framework of near-contact forms. We prove a Gray-type stability theorem for the kernels of near-contact forms, which we call near-contact structures, and derive a local neighborhood theorem around their singularities. As applications, we obtain a stability result for the contact topology of curl eigenforms, a classification of near-contact structures in terms of formal homotopical data, and a proof of the existence of contact structures on closed orientable 3-manifolds based on the spectral theory of the curl operator. We also study the symplectization of curl eigenforms as a possible bridge to symplectic dynamics. Finally, we characterize those transversely vanishing $1$-forms which are curl eigenforms for some smooth Riemannian metric. We deduce a generalization of the classical correspondence, due to Etnyre and Ghrist, between Reeb-like fields of contact forms and nowhere-vanishing rotational Beltrami fields to the setting of transversely vanishing vector fields.

## The reverse isoperimetric inequality for convex shapes included in a triangle

Rida Ait El Manssour、Zakaria Fattah、Ilias Ftouhi

固定三角形 T 和面积比例 η∈(0,1)，作者在 K⊂T、面积为 η|T| 的凸体中确定周长最大者并刻画等号。每个最大者仍是三角形：保留 T 的一条最长边，第三顶点置于一条最短边上由面积约束确定的位置，从而得到用 η 及边长表示的显式最大周长。

We establish a sharp reverse isoperimetric inequality for convex subsets of a triangle under a prescribed area constraint. Given a triangle $T$ and an area fraction $\eta\in(0,1)$, we determine the set with maximal perimeter among convex bodies $K\subset T$ satisfying $|K|=\eta|T|$ and characterize the equality cases. Every maximizer is a triangle, and the optimal configuration is obtained by retaining a longest side of $T$ and placing the third vertex on a shortest side, at the position determined by the area constraint. This characterization yields an explicit expression for the maximal perimeter in terms of $\eta$ and the side lengths of $T$.

## An approximate inner ball condition characterizing $C^{1,α}$ convex bodies

Anthony R. Cappello

对 0<α≤1，作者以近似内球条件刻画 C^(1,α) 凸体：对每个边界点 x 及充分小 ε，有半径 C ε^((1−α)/(1+α)) 的内球，其与 x 的距离至多 ε。由此给出强制性 C^(1,α)_loc 函数之凸包络保持局部 C^(1,α) 正则性的新证明，并证明同正则凸体与非空闭凸集的 Minkowski 和仍为同正则凸域。

The main result of this paper uses an approximate inner ball condition to characterize $C^{1,\alpha}$ convex bodies for $0<\alpha\leq 1$. In particular, we show that a convex body $K$ is of class $C^{1,\alpha}$ if and only if there exist constants $C,\varepsilon_0>0$ such that for every $0<\varepsilon\leq\varepsilon_0$ and for every $x\in\partial K$ there exists $h^\varepsilon(x)$ satisfying $B(h^\varepsilon(x), C\varepsilon^\frac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha})\subset K$ and $\operatorname{dist}(x,B(h^\varepsilon(x), C\varepsilon^\frac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha}))\leq\varepsilon.$ We then give a new proof of the regularity of the convex envelope by showing that $\operatorname{conv}(f)\in C^{1,\alpha}_{\rm loc}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ whenever $f\in C^{1,\alpha}_{\rm loc}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ and $f$ satisfies the coercivity condition $f(x)\to\infty$ as $|x|\to\infty$. Finally, we use the approximate inner ball characterization of $C^{1,\alpha}$ convex bodies to prove that the Minkowski sum of a $C^{1,\alpha}$ convex body and a nonempty closed convex set is a $C^{1,\alpha}$ convex domain.

## Filling knots and essential geodesic systole

Christopher J. Leininger、Yandi Wu

We prove that every closed, orientable $3$--manifold contains hyperbolic knots with arbitrarily large essential geodesic systole. That is, for any $3$--manifold $M$, and $R> 0$ there is a hyperbolic knot $K \subset M$ such that every closed geodesic in the knot complement which is non-null-homotopic in $M$ has length at least $R$.
